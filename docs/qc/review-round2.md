# QC review round 2: rebuilt Akkadian, Sumerian, Hesiod and Near Eastern collections

Independent reviewer, 2026-11-12. This file is the only one written in the repo; no project file was edited.

## Scope and method

- 25 collections: the 13 eBL Akkadian collections (gilgamesh, enuma-elish, atrahasis, descent-of-ishtar, erra-and-ishum, anzu, etana, adapa, ludlul, theodicy, great-hymn-to-shamash, counsels-of-wisdom, cuthaean-legend), 6 Sumerian (sumerian-gilgamesh-huwawa, lament-for-ur, inanas-descent, instructions-of-shuruppak, exaltation-of-inana, enki-and-ninhursag), hesiod-theogony, hesiod-shield, hesiod-works-and-days, cyrus-cylinder, mesha-stele, ketef-hinnom.
- Per collection, 2 sections whose witness, lines or page edges changed in the fixes (docs/qc/fixes-*.md and the "QC fix round" part of each docs/collections/<key>.md), plus 1 drawn at random (python `random.seed('20261112-round2')`). Where the random draw repeated a changed section, the next page was taken. Some collections got extra sections. In all, about 85 sections were checked by eye.
- Each check:
  - **a scan:** the image was opened, cropped or zoomed, and compared with the claims: the object (ink or museum number where visible), the face, the column and row structure, and the first and last line. Cuneiform breaks were matched to the brackets. For Hesiod, the first and last verse rows were read on Laur. Plut. 32.16, which writes two verses per row.
  - **b alignment:** the original and the English cover the same lines and labels, with no commentary text and no raw ATF.
  - **c gaps/notes:** a sample of each scan_note's "not preserved", "entirely broken" and "missing" claims was checked against the original text, the cached ATF and eBL data, and the photo.
  - **d render:** markup was checked, and the rendered page where useful.
- Scripted checks over every section of the sampled collections looked for leftover eBL codes (`!bs !cm !qt !zz ($___$) &2 {( )} °\°`), escaped or stray markup, label parity between the two columns, and scan files that exist.
- `python3 scripts/check_collection.py <key>` for all 25: **0 hard failures each**. The only warnings are gilgamesh tablet-v §278 (older than this round) and mesha-stele (one image for the stele).
- The work was split across four reviewers running the same procedure. Four of the reported defects were then re-checked directly (marked "re-checked" below).

## Result

231 check rows: **205 OK, 19 DOUBT, 7 DEFECT**. The corrections the fixers named all hold: the Hesiod page edges (Theogony 246v/247r, Shield 248v/249r), the W&D 502-503 move, 370-372 and 378 at page feet, W&D 120 and 169 absent, the eBL re-witnessing (commentaries gone, raw codes written out), the Cyrus 23a/26a/30a clauses, Mesha's RTL plus transliteration lines, and the Ketef Hinnom detail image. The new defects are in scan notes and in single-line placement.
