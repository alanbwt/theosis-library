# QC review: Near Eastern and Egyptian collections

Independent reviewer, 2026-11-12. Nothing in the repo was edited except this file.

## Scope and method

- 41 collections: proto-cuneiform, sumerian-king-list, eridu-genesis, enki-and-ninhursag, instructions-of-shuruppak, kesh-temple-hymn, inanas-descent, sumerian-gilgamesh-huwawa, exaltation-of-inana, lament-for-ur, gilgamesh, enuma-elish, atrahasis, descent-of-ishtar, erra-and-ishum, anzu, etana, adapa, ludlul, theodicy, great-hymn-to-shamash, counsels-of-wisdom, cuthaean-legend, code-of-hammurabi, berossus, black-obelisk, papyrus-prisse, papyrus-westcar, pyramid-texts-unas, rosetta-stone, manetho, herodotus-egypt, diodorus-egypt, mesha-stele, siloam-inscription, gezer-calendar, tel-dan-stele, ketef-hinnom, sanchuniathon, cyrus-cylinder, vendidad.
- Per collection, 3 sections: the first section of the first entry, the middle section of the middle entry, and one more at random (seed 20261112). Single-section collections give 1 section and vendidad gives 2, so **115 sections** were checked by hand.
- For each picked section: (a) the scan was opened and compared with the scan_note (object, face, column, page, first/last words, line count, breaks); (b) original and English were compared for the same lines; (c) the rendered page was fetched from http://localhost:8765/library/<id>.html; (d) the introduction, notes, verification and docs/collections/<key>.md were checked against the data.
- Automated checks over **all 1,717 rendered sections** of 40 collections (vendidad is not built locally): non-empty columns, image present, no escaped markup; line-label recount for every "This page shows N of the M lines" claim (42 entries); duplicate-image and duplicate-text scans; museum-number vs scan-file-name match.
- `python3 scripts/check_collection.py <key>` for all 41: 0 hard failures each.

Verdicts in the table: 398 OK, 46 DOUBT, 23 DEFECT (one row per check per section, plus systemic rows at the top).

## Systemic findings

1. **Commentary, quotation and letter tablets shown as copies of the poem (eBL importer).** The importer picks "the photographed manuscript with the most signs preserved" for each line. Commentary tablets win that test because they add glosses. In 60 sections across 11 entries the Akkadian column holds a commentary's quoted lemma plus its explanations, while the English translates the whole poem line. Nothing on the page says the witness is a commentary. The same problem covers the K.1351 quotation in the Cuthaean Legend (line 2 names "Bēl-ēṭir son of Ibâ" beside the Legend's English) and the K.4267 letter in the Theodicy. The list is in the appendix.
2. **Raw eBL ATF codes on the page.** `!bs`, `!cm`, `!qt`, `!zz`, `($___$)`, `&2`, `{( )}`, `°x\<..>°` and the `&` half-line divider are printed with no key; Enuma Elish VII alone has 282 `!bs` and 221 `($___$)`. The intros explain only [ ], x, #, ? and {det}.
3. **ETCSL/CDLI importer drops lines that are on the photographed tablets.** CDLI's `#link: def A = Q...` / `>>A n` link syntax (present in all 30 cached Gilgamesh-Huwawa ATF files) is ignored, and the fallback fuzzy match (>=0.6) fails on Emesal and variant spellings. Lines that are present on the tablet are then reported as "not preserved" or "on no photographed tablet". Confirmed: Gilgamesh-Huwawa A 2 is on P343683 obv i 2 but omitted; also Inana's Descent 384 and Lament for Ur K244 and K249. Coverage figures for these collections are understated.
4. **English translates more than the tablet holds.** The scan_note boilerplate warns only about bracketed restorations. Unbracketed English often renders lines the photographed tablet does not preserve (e.g. Atrahasis SB II 110). Spec rule 7 asks for this to be said.
5. **Outdated gap reasons.** Gaps are still explained as "photographs not openly licensed" (Berlin, Philadelphia, Yale, even British Museum Ipiq-Aya tablets). Spec rule 9 (2026-11-12) accepts restrictive photo terms, and the Gilgamesh collection already uses Yale CC0 images.
6. **Vendidad is not in the local build** (404 for both entries and the collection page). Fargard 2 has only the one-line short form for §12-14 in the Avestan, while the English gives the full text.
7. **Translation licence.** The Tel Dan stele uses Biran and Naveh 1995 (in copyright) "by the owner's decision"; spec rule 6 says translation licensing is unchanged.
8. **Single whole-object images** (Mesha, Siloam, Ketef Hinnom, Unas walls, Westcar frames): the mapping is right, but on faint or oblique photos a reader cannot find a given line. Not a defect under the spec, but the promise is weak there.
