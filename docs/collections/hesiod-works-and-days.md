## Sources

- Manuscript: Florence, BML, Plut. 32.16 (late 13th century), Works and Days on fols. 233r-239v, with scholia in the margins (Pinakes gives "235-241", which is not right: the poem begins on 233r). BML Teca Digitale IIIF, manifest `https://cdm21059.contentdm.oclc.org/iiif/plutei:596036/manifest.json`; CC BY-NC-SA.
- Greek: Evelyn-White, Loeb 1914, Perseus `tlg0020.tlg002.perseus-grc2` (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- English: Evelyn-White 1914 (public domain), Perseus `tlg0020.tlg002.perseus-eng2`.

## Choice of witness

Older manuscripts exist. Paris gr. 2771 (10th/11th c.) is on Gallica, which returned a Cloudflare block to every automated request during this work. Laur. Plut. 31.39 (12th c.) is online in the same Teca Digitale (manifest `plutei:696851`), but its opening leaves are badly faded and covered with interlinear glosses in the photographs, so the first and last verse of every page could not be read with certainty. Plut. 32.16 is legible throughout and carries all three Hesiodic poems in one hand, so it was used. Replacing it with 31.39 later would be an upgrade if those leaves can be read.

## Mapping method

As for the Theogony: two verses to a row, first and last verse rows of every page read on the image, continuity checked at every page break. Page ranges: 233r 1-58, 233v 59-117, 234r 118-183, 234v 184-239, 235r 240-301, 235v 302-361, 236r 362-414, 236v 415-474, 237r 475-536, 237v 537-598, 238r 599-660, 238v 661-724, 239r 725-775, 239v 776-828. English in five-line blocks placed by middle line.

## Differences from the edition, gaps

- 120 (ἀφνειοὶ μήλοισι...) is not on fol. 234r; it is missing from most medieval manuscripts.
- 169a-169d (papyrus lines on Cronos) are not in the medieval manuscripts and are left out.
- 360-363: the manuscript has the traditional order 360, 361, 362, 363; the Loeb moves 363 before 361. Sections follow line numbers, so 363 sits with fol. 236r.
- The edition has no line 310.

## Open questions

- The manuscript's text differs in wording here and there from Evelyn-White's; differences are not recorded verse by verse.
