# eridu-genesis

**Entry:** `verified-eridu-genesis-cbs-10673` (1 entry, 11 sections)

## Contents
The Sumerian Flood story (ETCSL 1.7.4) on Nippur tablet CBS 10673 (+ CBS 10867), Penn Museum,
CDLI P265876: all five surviving segments A-E (89 lines), i.e. everything ETCSL edits.

## Sources
- Photo: CDLI https://cdli.earth/dl/photo/P265876.jpg, cropped to obverse and reverse.
- Sumerian: ETCSL 1.7.4 composite (ETCSL's only source is CBS 10673; CDLI has no transliteration).
- English: ETCSL 1.7.4 translation, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0.

## Mapping
Column numbering from A. Poebel, Historical Texts (PBS 4/1, 1914) pp. 14-16 (public domain,
archive.org/details/historicaltexts00poeb), matched to ETCSL segments by opening/closing lines:
A = obv i, B = obv ii, C 1-16 = obv iii, C 17-28 = rev iv, D = rev v, E = rev vi.
Checked on the photo: obverse text at the foot of three columns, reverse text at the top just after
the bottom edge; reverse right-hand column (iv) has every line broken at the right, as C 17-28.

## Gaps
- About 32-38 lines lost in each break (noted in the English).
- Not included: the Ur fragment (published 2018, JCS 70) and Schøyen MS 3026 (unpublished); no open
  translation and not in ETCSL.
- ETCSL leaves line C 28 untranslated (noted).

## Open questions
- The joined fragment CBS 10867 is visible in the photo; ETCSL names only CBS 10673 as its source.
