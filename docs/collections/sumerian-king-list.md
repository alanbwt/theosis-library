# sumerian-king-list

**Entry:** `verified-sumerian-king-list-wb444` (1 entry, 21 sections)

## Contents
The Sumerian King List on the Weld-Blundell Prism (Ashmolean Museum AN1923.444, W-B 444, CDLI P384786),
all four faces and eight columns: the eight kings before the Flood (ETCSL lines 1-39), the Flood (line 39-40),
and the dynasties after it down to Sin-magir of Isin (lines 40-377), plus the scribe's colophon.

## Mapping
Sections are ETCSL translation paragraphs. Every prism line carries CDLI's own link to the composite
(`>>Q000371 nnn`), whose numbering was checked line by line against ETCSL 2.1.1. Each section lists the
prism lines in that paragraph's range, grouped by face and column, and points at the face crop(s).
Face order a-d in the CDLI photo was checked by eye against the broken areas in the transliteration
(a ii top 4 lines, b ii top 11, c i top 13, c ii top 5, d ii ends with double ruling + colophon).

## Gaps
- ETCSL paragraph 378-431 (closing totals) is not on this prism: not published.
- Broken lines at the tops of columns are shown in brackets as CDLI gives them.
