# p66: P66 (Papyrus Bodmer II), Gospel of John

Built by `scripts/import_p66.py` (shared machinery in `scripts/import_p52.py`; `--force` re-downloads images).

## Contents

148 sections (one per papyrus page, pages 1-34 and 39-152), 21 entries, one per chapter in which a page begins
(`verified-p66-john-1` ... `verified-p66-john-21`). Covered: John 1:1-6:11, 6:35-14:26 (complete pages), then
14:29-21:9 on damaged glass-mounted leaves (much of each page lost; the transcription supplies the lost letters in
brackets). Pages 35-38 (John 6:12-34) are lost.

- **Greek**: INTF NTVMR transcription of GA P66 (docID 10066), 148 page files, all CC BY 4.0.
- **English**: KJV (eBible.org USFM, public domain).

## Mapping method

NTVMR's page index links each page to its Bodmer Lab image number (1072205287_NNN). Every image carries a printed
caption naming the verses (plates: "I, 1-14"; glass leaves: "PB II Evangile de Jean, supplément 20, 25-26"); all 148
captions were read and are quoted in the `scan_note`s (stored in `data/sources/p66/visual_check.json`). For 76 of the
108 plates the last line of writing was also read and matched to the transcription; on the others the bottom of the
page is faint in the 1962 plates. Caption ranges differ from NTVMR's by a verse at a few page boundaries (for example
p. 21 "IV, 25-33" against NTVMR 4:24-33), because a verse that starts at the foot of one page is counted differently.

## Notes

- Verses P66 does not have (empty in the transcription): John 5:4, 7:53-8:11, 16:15. Noted on their pages and left out of
  the English. Verses lost in damaged parts of the later leaves are listed on each page.
- John 1:18 (page 2): P66 reads μονογενης θεος; noted.
- Two NTVMR files are defective (page 120 at John 16:29, page 128 at John 18:16: letters typed into the verse id); the
  Greek follows the file and the defect is noted on those pages.
- The scribe corrected his own work heavily; only the first hand is shown.
- The Chester Beatty (CBL BP XIX) and Cologne fragments of P66 are part of NTVMR's transcription where they belong;
  they were not photographed separately here.
