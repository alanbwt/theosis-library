# p75: P75 (Papyrus Hanna 1 (Mater Verbi)), Luke and John

Built by `scripts/import_p75.py` (shared machinery in `scripts/import_p52.py`; `--force` re-downloads images).

## Contents

96 sections (one per page side, NTVMR folios 7r-64v less six left out, see Gaps), 30 entries, one per chapter in
which a page begins (`verified-p75-luke-3` ... `verified-p75-john-14`). Titles give the exact verse range and folios.

Shown: Luke 3:18-22, 4:34-5:10, 6:11-7:32 (with damage), 7:35-18:18, 22:4-20, 22:37-24:53; John 1:1-10:14,
11:2-45, 11:48-57, 12:3-13:10, 14:8-26. Lines lost inside a page are listed in that page's `scan_note`. The papyrus
also preserves Luke 3:33-4:2, 5:37-6:4, 22:21-37, John 10:14-11:2 and 14:26-15:10 on the six pages left out (see Gaps).

## Mapping method

From frame 1A.4 on, the Vatican frames are in codex order: frame n (1A.4-1A.12, 1B.1-12, 2A.1-12, 2B.1-12, 3A.1-3)
holds the n-th surviving leaf, recto then verso, matched to the NTVMR folio pages in order. Anchor: the pencilled label
on frame 1A.4r reads "Luc VI: 16-28" = NTVMR folio 12r. The first three frames do not follow that order: frame 1A.1
holds two detached pieces of folio 7 (pencilled "Luc III, 19. 21-22"), frame 1A.2 the main fragment of folio 7 (front,
Luke 3:19-21) and folio 11 (back, Luke 6:12-14; pencilled "Luc V: 37-39; VI 2-4" for the other side), and frame 1A.3
is folio 9 (pencilled "Luc IV, 34-42"). These are set in `FRAME_OVERRIDE` in the importer. Every page was then
checked on the image: lines near the top and the foot of the writing were read (bands fetched at the detected top and
bottom of the ink) and compared with the transcription. Results are in `data/sources/p75/visual_check.json` and in
each `scan_note`. Frames 3A.4-3A.6 (small fragments) are not used.

## Gaps

- Left out because their text could not be matched to the photographs with certainty: folios 7v and 11r (the other
  sides of the scattered fragments in frames 1A.1-1A.2), 39v, 58r, 58v and 64v (frames 2A.3v, 2B.10r, 2B.10v, 3A.3v:
  too damaged or faded to read at the resolution checked). Their transcriptions are cached; a closer reading of the
  full-resolution images could restore them.
- Text lost from the papyrus itself: see the coverage list above.

## Notes

- Verses P75 does not have (empty verse elements in the transcription) are listed on the page and left out of the
  English: John 5:4, John 7:53-8:11, Luke 22:43-44, Luke 23:17.
- Readings noted on their pages: Luke 8:43, 9:54-56, 11:2-4 (short Lord's Prayer), 16:19 (the rich man named
  νευης), 23:34 (no "Father, forgive them"), John 1:18 (μονογενης θεος), John 10:7 (ποιμην).
- NTVMR labels two consecutive pages 38v and 39v; they are shown on frames 2A.3r and 2A.3v, a single leaf. The NTVMR
  folio labels are kept as section ids; the frame is named in each section.
