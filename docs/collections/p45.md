# p45: P45 (Chester Beatty Biblical Papyrus I), Gospels and Acts: NOT BUILT (importer ready)

Status: no published entries. `scripts/import_p45.py` works end to end (64 transcription pages cached, 60 page images
reachable), but the page-to-image mapping could not be confirmed by eye within this round, so its output was removed.

## What is in place

- **Greek**: INTF NTVMR transcription of GA P45 (docID 10045), 64 page files, all CC BY 4.0, cached in
  `data/sources/p45/ntvmr/10045/`. Verses P45 lacks per the transcription: Luke 12:9, Acts 8:37.
- **Photographs**: NTVMR's page index (`data/sources/p45/ntvmr/meta-10045.json`) links 60 pages to images served publicly
  (accessLevel 0) at `https://ntmss.info/images/webfriendly/10045/<file>`: Chester Beatty Library photographs
  ("Images © The Trustees of the Chester Beatty Library") of the leaves in their glass frames, plus the ÖNB photographs
  of the Vienna fragment Pap. G. 31974 (folio 2). The Chester Beatty online viewer's IIIF API refused automated access
  (HTTP 403) and was not used.
- No image is linked for the small John fragments (NTVMR pages Br, Cr, Bv, Cv).

## Why it was not published

- Folios 3-4 (Mark 4-6): NTVMR's four images for 3r, 3v, 4r, 4v are halves of single photographs of a frame holding both
  leaves, upside down; the frame labels ("Fol. 3R ... Mark IV.36-40", "Fol. 4R ... Mark V.38-VI.3") sit over the
  opposite halves from NTVMR's assignment, and the writing looks mirrored. The mapping needs a reading of the Greek.
- The frame labels do not follow one convention: on folio 1 each face shows "Fol. 1R"/"Fol. 1V" with the other face's
  verses written at the foot, while folio 9's labels read the other way round from NTVMR's assignment, and folio 8's
  match the page itself. So labels alone cannot confirm the mapping; each page needs its first lines read on the
  photograph (most lines survive only as a few letters).
