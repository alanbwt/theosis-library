## Sources

## Mapping

One face, shown whole in both photographs. Checked on the images: seven lines right to left, yrḥw at the start of line 1,
the ʿayin circle near the right end of line 2, short lines 6-7, and separate letters at the lower left corner.

## Limits

- The letters at the lower left corner (read by Lidzbarski as the beginning of a name) are mentioned but not given as text.
- Readings follow Lidzbarski/Driver 1913 (e.g. line 5 קצרו כל, where many later editions read קצר וכל).
