# enki-and-ninhursag

**Entry:** `verified-enki-and-ninhursag-cbs-4561` (1 entry, 38 sections)

## Contents
Enki and Ninhursaĝa (ETCSL 1.1.1) on Nippur tablet CBS 4561, Penn Museum, CDLI P260875: ETCSL
lines 1-281 except the lettered lines that CBS 4561 does not carry.

## Sources
- Photo: CDLI https://cdli.earth/dl/photo/P260875.jpg, cropped to obverse and reverse.
- Sumerian: ETCSL 1.1.1 composite (CDLI has no transliteration of CBS 4561); the composite follows
  CBS 4561 and fills breaks from UET 6/1 1 (Ur) and AO 6724 (Louvre).
- English: ETCSL 1.1.1 translation, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0.

## Mapping
Columns from S. Langdon, PBS 10/1 (1915) pp. 69-87 (public domain, transliteration by column),
first/last lines matched to ETCSL: obv i = 1-43, obv ii = 44-87, obv iii = 88-132, rev iv = 142-178,
rev v = 179-230, rev vi = 235-281. Checked on the photo: obverse lower left corner broken (end of
col. i), reverse worn along the top of all three columns (Langdon: 5-12 lines lost at each head).

## Gaps
- Not published (not on CBS 4561; the Ur and Louvre tablets have no usable CDLI photo):
  ETCSL paragraphs 49A-49P, 49Q-49V (lands sending goods to Dilmun), 126A-126K, 126L-126Q,
  126R-126CC (a further generation of Enki's daughters).
- Breaks on CBS 4561 (37-43, 142-146, 179-190, 235-246) noted in the sections.

## Open questions
- Line-level placement inside the worn reverse heads rests on Langdon's 1915 readings; ETCSL may
  read a few lines there from the Ur tablet rather than CBS 4561.

## QC fixes (2026-11-12)
ETCSL's composite has gap rows where it gives no transliteration ('3 lines missing' after 36, '9 lines fragmentary' after 132, '4 lines missing' after 159, '2 lines fragmentary' after 187, '4 lines fragmentary' after 230, '3 lines fragmentary' after 241). They were dropped silently, so e.g. section 127-146 jumped from 132 to 142. Each gap is now shown in the Sumerian as a lacuna with its ETCSL line numbers (e.g. '(9 lines fragmentary: ETCSL lines 133-141, no transliteration)', at the broken head of reverse column iv) and named in the section note. The shared ETCSL/CDLI engine in the importer was also updated (alias links, order-preserving alignment); this entry does not use CDLI witnesses, so nothing else changed.
