# Black Obelisk of Shalmaneser III (BM 118885): the five epigraphs

Collection key: `black-obelisk` · importer: `scripts/import_black_obelisk.py` · shelf: Mesopotamia

## Contents (1 entry, 5 sections)

`verified-black-obelisk-epigraphs`: epigraphs 1-5 (RIMA 3 A.0.102.87-91), one section each; epigraph 2 is the tribute of
Jehu of Israel.

## Sources

- **Akkadian and English**: ORACC RIAo Shalmaneser III 087-091 (Q004692-Q004696), after Grayson, RIMA 3 (1996); CC BY-SA 3.0
  per page credits (the RIAo JSON metadata says CC0). Pages saved in `data/sources/black-obelisk/`.
- **Photographs**: Szilas, British Museum 2010, sides A-D (CC BY-SA 4.0), each cropped to the five relief registers with the
  epigraph lines; Osama Shukir Muhammed Amin 2014 (CC BY-SA 4.0), side A register 2 (Jehu) close up.

## Mapping

Epigraph n is the line directly above register n, running round all four faces ("each scene occupies four panels around the
monument and is described by a cuneiform script above them", Wikipedia). Checked on the photographs: the scenes match the
epigraphs (register 1 prostrate ruler, horses, camels; 2 Jehu under the parasol; 3 camels, water buffalo, elephant, monkeys;
4 lions and stag; 5 tribute bearers), and a line of signs stands above each register on every face; the Jehu close-up shows
the epigraph line turning the corner.

## Gaps

- The 190-line annals text (RIMA 3 A.0.102.14, ORACC Q004619, CDLI P423554) is not published. Neither ORACC nor CDLI divides
  its lines by face (CDLI ATF has a single `@surface a`), no open source gives the face-by-face line division, and the face
  photographs (about 1,000 px wide) are too small to fix line starts by eye. With a high-resolution photograph of each face
  (top and bottom zones) and a reading of the first signs of each face, it could be added.
- The epigraph lines are small on the face crops; the signs cannot be read one by one there.
