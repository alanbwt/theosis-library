## Rendering of the Greek (all papyrus collections)

Line by line as transcribed (`<br>` at each line beginning). First hand only. `<supplied>` letters in square brackets
(adjacent bracketed words merged into one bracket), `<unclear>` letters with a dot below, `<gap>` as `[...]`,
nomina sacra and numerals with a combining overline (U+0305), as in the Akhmim codex collection. Words broken at a
line end stay broken. `<note>` elements are omitted. Book titles (incipit / inscriptio / subscriptio / explicit) are
shown in italics on their own lines and have no English. Verse numbers as `<sup>`: `c:v` at the first verse of a page
and at a chapter change, otherwise `v`; the English uses the same labels.

## Mapping method

NTVMR's page index (folio 001r = John 18:31-33, 001v = 18:37-38, linked to the Rylands images jrl020153tr / jrl020154tr)
and the Manchester catalogue (recto 18:31-33, verso 18:37-38). All seven lines on each side were read on the images and
are listed in each section's `scan_note`.

## Gaps and notes

- About eleven of each side's eighteen lines are lost; most of every surviving line is restored in brackets by the
  transcribers. The English is the complete KJV verse.
- The restoration of 18:37 (whether the second εις τουτο fits the line) is debated; the NTVMR restoration is shown.
