## Mapping
Sections are ETCSL translation paragraphs. Tablet lines are placed by CDLI's own links to composite Q000782 (ETCSL numbering). Every link is scored against the ETCSL composite text (sign-sequence similarity): links under 0.25 on lines with 3+ signs are dropped; links off by a line or two (a match >= 0.5 and 0.25 better within +/-3 lines) are moved to the matching ETCSL line; links must run in composite order. Tablets without CDLI links are aligned by sign-sequence matching (>= 0.6, clear margin over the next best line, 3+ signs, longest run in composite order). Per paragraph the tablets carrying most lines are chosen (max 4); a paragraph is shown only if they carry at least half of its lines. Tablets catalogued with a cast, or whose photo has a long side under 1200 px, are not used. Every photograph used was looked at (montages) to confirm it shows the tablet itself.

## Gaps
Paragraphs left out: 73-75, 272-273. The Early Dynastic versions (Abu Salabikh, Adab, c. 2600-2500 BCE) differ from the OB text and have no open translation; not included.
