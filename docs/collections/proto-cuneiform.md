## Sources
- Photos: CDLI, whole tablet, never upscaled; non-commercial use.
- Original: CDLI proto-cuneiform ATF (sz shown as š; numerals as N-signs).
- English: CDLI's own line-by-line translations (#tr.en) in the same ATF. CDLI terms allow re-use of
  transliterations and translations with reference to CDLI (treated as explicit open-access terms, spec rule 6).
  The translator is not named in the ATF; credited as "CDLI".

## Mapping
Sections = faces/columns of each tablet as given in the ATF; one photo per tablet; each photo checked by eye
(MSVO 3, 29: inscribed obverse, blank reverse, as CDLI records).

## Gaps / choice
Only tablets with photo + transliteration + English in CDLI were taken (survey of MSVO 1, 1-32 and MSVO 3,
1-35: MSVO 1, 5+6 has no photo). Uruk IV tablets (c. 3300 BCE) mostly lack English in CDLI; not included.

## Open questions
- Proto-cuneiform "translations" are interpretations of bookkeeping entries; the introduction says so.
- Jemdet Nasr provenience is marked "uncertain (mod. Jemdet Nasr)" by CDLI; the intros say "attributed".
