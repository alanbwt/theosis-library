## Mapping method

- Vatican: the typed label on each frame gives page number(s) and verses (e.g. "VIII verso = p. 22 (KB) 1 Petri 5, 12-14
  ... p. 23 (KΓ) 2 Petri 1, 1-5"); these agree with NTVMR, whose "folio" for P72 is the papyrus page number. The labels
  are transcribed in `FRAME_LABEL` in the importer and quoted in each `scan_note`.
- Bodmer: the printed caption under each plate ("PB VII Epître de Jude, 4-6" etc.); page 68 (canvas 055) has no caption
  but carries the page number ξη and the end of Jude.
- Every page was then viewed and its first and last legible lines compared with the transcription; the checks are in
  `data/sources/p72/visual_check.json` and appended to each `scan_note`.

## Notes and things to know

- The NTVMR transcription of page 1 lacks the title line πετρου επιστολη α at the head of the page (noted on the page).
- The colophon ειρηνη τω γραψαντι και τω αναγινωσκοντι after 1 Peter (page 22) and 2 Peter (page 36) is recorded by NTVMR
  only in an editorial note, so it is not in the Greek column (noted on both pages).
- Page 62 begins with the last lines of the 11th Ode of Solomon (not transcribed by NTVMR; noted).
- Page 66: a corrector's line at the foot (Jude 16) is not shown, since the Greek is the first hand (noted).
- The verse-level comparison found no KJV verse missing from P72 inside any page's range; differences are in wording
  only, which the KJV does not reflect.
- The Vatican page crops are about 1120 x 1580 px (the full resolution of each half of a 2882 px frame image); not
  upscaled.
