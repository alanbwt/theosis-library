# Siloam inscription (Istanbul 2195 T)

Collection key: `siloam-inscription` · importer: `scripts/import_siloam_inscription.py` · shelf: Levant & Israel

## Contents (1 entry, 1 section)

`verified-siloam-inscription-istanbul-2195`: all six lines in one section. Complete as preserved.

## Sources

- **Hebrew**: S. R. Driver, *Notes on the Hebrew Text and the Topography of the Books of Samuel*, 2nd ed. (Oxford 1913),
  p. ix, the inscription "transliterated into modern Hebrew characters" (public domain). Transcribed by hand from the
  archive.org page image (notesonthehebrew00drivuoft, leaf n38) into
  `data/sources/siloam-inscription/driver1913_siloam_verified.txt`. Driver's overlined (uncertain) letters are kept with a
  combining overline; his asterisks (lost letters) and brackets are kept. The Latin line is a mechanical letter-for-letter
  romanization of Driver's square script made by the importer (it adds no reading).
- **English**: Driver 1913, pp. ix-x, printed line for line with the stone (leaves n38-n39), in
  `driver1913_siloam_english.txt`.
- **Photographs**: Wikimedia Commons, ywpark2003 (2010) and oncenawhile (2014), both CC0, of the panel in Istanbul,
  cropped to the stone. A Matson (LOC) photograph was not used because it may show a cast.

## Mapping

One inscribed panel shown whole in both photographs; checked on the images (panel, cracks and reset pieces at the left,
faint letters right to left across the middle). Line numbers are Driver's.

## Limits

- The letters are faint in the museum photographs; the six lines cannot be read letter by letter on them.
- Driver's reading is of 1913; later editions differ in places (e.g. zdh, line 3).

## QC fixes (2026-11-12)
The reviewer could not find the text on the photographs. No sharper open photograph of the stone exists on Wikimedia Commons (the larger Library of Congress Matson images show the PEF facsimile from a squeeze, or other inscriptions). The scan_note now says where the text is: the six lines run across the whole panel from the right-hand edge, line 1 (damaged) at the top through the crack network, its left end lost at the broken top-left corner, line 6 near the foot; the plaster-filled gap and crack a quarter of the way in from the left cut all lines; with contrast raised the letters show as rows of short strokes in the middle of the panel.
