## Mapping
By eye: the first and last words of both columns of fols. 174r, 174v, 175r, 175v, 176r and the
opening of 176v (to the end of 683a on line 24) were read and located in Burnet's text. Each section
lists the page(s) and column(s) in its `scan_note`; six sections cross a page break and list both pages.

## Gaps
- None within 676a-683a. The rest of book 3 (683b-702e, Sparta, Persia, Athens) is not included.

## Open questions
- Bury's marginal Stephanus letters do not always fall at the same word as Burnet's; the English
  sections follow Bury's letters, so a sentence may sit in the neighbouring section.
- The Greek is the edition, not a transcription of the page.
