# p46: P46 (Chester Beatty Biblical Papyrus II + P.Mich. inv. 6238), letters of Paul

Built by `scripts/import_p46.py` (shared machinery in `scripts/import_p52.py`; `--force` re-downloads images).
Only pages with a recorded visual check (`data/sources/p46/visual_check.json`) are published; the importer prints
the rest.

## Contents

One section per page side, grouped into one entry per chapter in which a page begins, in the codex's order of
letters (Romans, Hebrews, 1-2 Corinthians, Ephesians, Galatians, Philippians, Colossians, 1 Thessalonians).
154 sections (page sides) in 70 entries. Covered: Romans 5:17-6:14, 8:15-14:8, 15:11-16:23 (with the doxology after
15:33); Hebrews (complete except damaged lines); 1 Corinthians, 2 Corinthians, Ephesians, Galatians, Philippians,
Colossians, 1 Thessalonians 1:1-2:3 and 5:5-28, as far as the leaves survive, less the unpublished pages below.

## Sources

- **Greek**: INTF NTVMR transcription of GA P46 (docID 10046), 172 page files, all CC BY 4.0, cached in
  `data/sources/p46/ntvmr/10046/`.
- **Photographs**: the images NTVMR links to each page in its page index (`meta-10046.json`), served publicly
  (accessLevel 0) at `https://ntmss.info/images/webfriendly/10046/<file>`: Chester Beatty Library photographs of the
  Dublin leaves in their frames (files `T000....jpg`), University of Michigan Library photographs of the Ann Arbor
  leaves (files `10046x6238_..._UMich.jpg`, with U-M's printed label "P. Mich. Inv. 6238 Epistles of Paul ...").
  Where NTVMR also links an INTF image (`10046_NNN_INTF.jpg`) the holding library's photograph is used. Reduced to
  2400 px on the long side. Terms: none stated beyond the attributions in NTVMR (rule 9).
- Not used: the University of Michigan APIS server (quod.lib.umich.edu answered with a Cloudflare challenge; rule 8)
  and CSNTM's IIIF server (returned empty or broken images).
- **English**: KJV (eBible.org USFM, public domain).

## Mapping method

NTVMR's page index links each folio to its photograph. Each page was then checked on the image by one or more of:
the scribe's page number at the head of the page (Greek numerals; consecutive across the leaves checked, e.g. Romans
11:35 on folio 16r carries λ = 30, Hebrews 11:35 on folio 35r ξη = 68, Galatians 3:2 on folio 83r ρξβ = 162); the
Chester Beatty frame discs and labels ("P. II 35R", "Rom. V.17-VI.3"); U-M's printed label; and the first line of
writing compared with the transcription. The page numbers show that NTVMR's folio numbers are not the scribe's leaf
count throughout (folios 16-40: page = 2 x folio - 2; folios 83-88: page = 2 x folio - 4), so numerals are recorded
as read, not computed.

## Notes

- P46 places the doxology Romans 16:25-27 after 15:33 (folio 20r); the English follows the papyrus order and the page
  says so.
- Verses the transcription marks as not written: 2 Corinthians 8:20. The papyrus passes from υφ ημων in 8:19 to
  8:21 (8:20 ends with the same words); noted on the page. The KJV of 8:19 is given in full.
- Folios 18r-18v (Romans 14:9-15:10) have no image linked in NTVMR and are not built.
- Not published (16 pages, image downloaded but no page number, label or line could be read with certainty at the
  scale checked): folios 11v, 13v, 42v, 49v, 50v, 51v, 59v, 88v, 89r, 89v, 90v, 91r, 91v, 93v, 94r, 94v. Most are
  badly broken Chester Beatty leaves near the end of the codex. Their images were removed from the scans folder; adding a check to
  `visual_check.json` and re-running the importer downloads and publishes them.
- The scribe's page numbers jump by two between folio 49r (ϙς = 96) and folio 52v (ρα = 101); the Chester Beatty
  frame discs on folios 50-53 agree with NTVMR's folio numbers, so the jump is in the numbering, not in the mapping.
- Hebrews 13:20-1 Corinthians 1:3 (folio 38r) and other pages where a letter begins or ends carry the book titles
  (προς εβραιους, προς κορινθιους α, ...), shown in italics.
