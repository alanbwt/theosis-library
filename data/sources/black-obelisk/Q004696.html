Shalmaneser III 091

Obverse
11

ma-da-tu šá mqar-pa-ru-un-da KUR.pa-ti-na-a-a KÙ.BABBAR.MEŠ KÙ.GI.MEŠ AN.NA.MEŠ ZABAR ar-ḫu ÚTUL.MEŠ ZABAR ZÚ.MEŠ AM.SI.MEŠ GIŠ.ESI.MEŠ am-ḫur-šu

(1) I received the payment of Qalparunda of the land Pattinu: silver, gold, tin, bronze compound (lit. “fast bronze”), bronze casseroles, ivory, (and) ebony.
