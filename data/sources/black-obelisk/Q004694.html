Shalmaneser III 089

Obverse
11

ma-da-tu šá KUR.mu-uṣ-ri ANŠE.A.AB.BA.MEŠ šá šu-na-a-a ṣe-ri-ši-na al-ap ÍD sa-de-e-ia su-ú-su pi-ra-a-ti.MEŠ ba-gi*-a-ti ú-qup-pu*.MEŠ am-ḫur-šu

(1) I received the payment of Egypt: two-humped camels, a water buffalo (lit. “a river ox”), a rhinoceros, an antelope, female elephants, female monkeys, (and) apes.
