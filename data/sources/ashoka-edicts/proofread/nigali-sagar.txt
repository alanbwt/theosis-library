@edict Nigali-Sagar | p. 165 | leaf 340
1 (A) Devānaṁpiyena Piyadasina lājina chodasa-vasā[bh]i[si]t[e]n[a]
2 Budhasa Konākamanasa thube dutiyaṁ vaḍhite
3 (B) . . . . . . . . sābhisitena cha atana āgācha mahīyite
4 . . . . . . . . . . pāpite
@en
(A) When king Dēvānāṁpriya Priyadarśin had been anointed fourteen years, he enlarged the <em>Stūpa</em> of the Buddha Kōnākamana to the double (of its original size).
(B) And when he had been anointed [twenty] years, he came himself and worshipped (this spot) [and] caused [a stone pillar to be set up].
@end
