§ 234.

@ If a boatman build a boat of 60 GUR for a man, he shall give
to him two shekels of silver as his wage.

§ 235.
@ Ifa boatman build a boat for a man and he do not make its
construction seaworthy and that boat meet with a disaster in the
same year in which it was put into commission, the boatman shall
reconstruct that boat and he shall strengthen it at his own
expense and he shall give the boat when strengthened to the
owner of the boat.
