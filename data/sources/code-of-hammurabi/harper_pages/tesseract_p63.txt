§ 172. 4

@ If her husband have not given her a gift, they shall make good
her dowry and she shall receive from the goods of her husband's
house a portion corresponding to that of a son. If her children
scheme to drive her out of the house, the judges shall inquire into
her antecedents and if the children be in the wrong, she shall
not go out from her husband’s house. If the woman set her
face to go out, she shall leave to her children the gift which her
husband gave her; she shall receive the dowry of her father’s
house, and the husband of her choice may take her.
