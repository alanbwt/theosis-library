§ 253.

@ If a man hire a man to oversee his farm and furnish him the
seed-grain and intrust him with oxen and contract with him to
cultivate the field, and that man steal either the seed or the crop
and it be found in his possession, they shall cut off his fingers.

S 254,

@ If he take the seed-grain and overwork the oxen, he shall
restore the quantity of grain which he has hoed.

@ If he let the oxen of the man on hire, or steal the seed-grain

and there be no crop in the field, they shall call that man to
account and he shall measure out 60 GUR of grain per 10 GAN.
