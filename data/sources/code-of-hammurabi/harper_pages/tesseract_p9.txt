TRANSLATION 9

Nineveh: the exalted one, who makes supplication to the great
gods; the descendant of Sumulailu, the powerful son of Sinmu-
ballit (Col. V), the ancient seed of royalty, the powerful king,
the Sun of Babylon, who caused light to go forth over the lands
of Sumer and Akkad; the king, who caused the four quarters of
the world to render obedience; the favorite of Nana,am JI. When
Marduk sent me to rule the people and to bring help to the
country, I established law and justice in the land and promoted
the welfare of the people.
