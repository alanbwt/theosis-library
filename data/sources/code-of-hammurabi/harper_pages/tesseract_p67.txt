S$ 179.

@ If (there be) a priestess or a devotee to whom her father has
given a dowry and written a deed of gift; if in the deed which
he has written for her, he have written ‘‘after her (death) she
may give to whomsoever she may please,” and he have granted her
full discretion; after her father dies she may give it to whom-
soever she may please after her (death). Her brothers may not
lay claim against her.

$ 180.

@ If a father do not give a dowry to his daughter, a bride or
devotee, after her father dies she shall receive as her share in
the goods of her father’s house the portion of a son, and she
shall enjoy it as long as she lives. After her (death) it belongs
to her brothers.
