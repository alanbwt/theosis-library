§ 141.

@_ If the wife of a man who is living in his house, set her face
to go out and play the part of a fool, neglect her house, belittle
her husband, they shall call her to account; if her husband say
“I have put her away,” he shall let her go. On her departure
nothing shall be given to her for her divorce. If her husband
say: “I have not put her away,” her husband may take another
woman. The first woman shall dwell in the house of her hus-
band as a maid servant.
