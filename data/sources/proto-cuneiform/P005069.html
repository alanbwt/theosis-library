obverse
1.a1. [4(N34)] 5(N14)# , [1(N57)] GAL~a SZAB~a
 en: 290 (ninda), the length, of the chief exchange agent,

 en: 100, the width:
1.b1. 1(N50) 6(N14) , GAN2
 en: 16 bur3 field,

 en: 2 eše3 perimeter;
  calculation: 290 × 100 = 29,000 sar, = 16 bur3 + 2 iku (?)
2.a1. 5(N34) 1(N14) 2(N01) , [1(N57)] PA~a# GIR3@g~b
 en: 312 (ninda), the length, of the equid herder,
2.a2. 1(N34) 3(N14)# , 1(N58)#
 en: 90, the width:
2.b1. 1(N50) 5(N14) , GAN2#
 en: 15 bur3 field,
2.b2. 1(N22) 4(N01) 7(N08) ,
 en: 1 eše3 4 7/10 iku;
  calculation: 312 × 90 = 28,080 sar, = 15 bur3 + 10 8/10 iku
3.a1. 4(N34) 5(N14) , 1(N57) NAM2 DI
 en: 290 (ninda), the length, of the chief justice,
3.a2. 1(N34) 3(N14) 3(N01) , 1(N58)
 en: 93, the width:
3.b. 1(N50) 5(N14) , GAN2
 en: 15 bur3 field,
  calculation: 290 × 93 = 26,970 sar, = 15 bur3 less 3/10 iku
4.a1. 4(N34) 5(N14) , 1(N57) ME~a
 en: 290 (ninda), the length, of the priest,
4.a2. 1(N34) 2(N01) , 1(N58)
 en: 62, the width:
4.b. 1(N50) 2(N08) , GAN2
 en: 10 bur3 2/10 iku field,
  calculation: 290 × 62 = 17,980 sar, = 10 bur3 less 2/10 iku (?)
5.a1. 5(N34) , [1(N57)] EN~c SAL
 en: 300 (ninda), the length, of the wife of the lord,
5.a2. 1(N34) 5(N14) , 1(N58)
 en: 110, the width:
5.b1. 1(N50) 8(N14) , GAN2
 en: 18 bur3 field,
5.b2. 1(N14) 2(N22) , [...]
 en: 1 bur3 2 eše3 ...
  calculation: 300 × 110 = 31,900 sar, = 18 bur3 6 iku
reverse
column 1
1. 2(N45) 3(N50) 4(N14) , EN~a GAN2
 en: 2 šar2 34 bur3 field of the lord;

2. 1(N45) 1(N50) 7(N14) , KI BU~a
 en: 1 šar2 17 bur3 field, measured;
  calculation in bur3: 16 + 15 + 15 + 10 + 17 = 73 (?; using “correct” calculations, not entries to text)

 en: 1 bur3 2 ee3 5 iku, wooded perimeter land;

1. 3(N45) 5(N50) 2(N14) 2(N22) 5(N01) , GAN2 AB~a |NI~a.RU| IL# [...]
