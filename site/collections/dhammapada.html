Dhammapada

c. 3rd century BCE (compilation); manuscript undated (Sinhalese palm leaf, Manchester Pali MS 19)

The complete Dhammapada, 423 verses in 26 chapters: the Pali text and Max Müller's 1881 English, each verse on the page of a Sinhalese palm-leaf manuscript in the John Rylands Library, Manchester (Pali MS 19), that carries it.
