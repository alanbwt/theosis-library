Diodorus Siculus on the gods of Egypt

c. 60-30 BCE (Diodorus); mid-10th c. (Vat. gr. 130)

Diodorus of Sicily's account of the Egyptian traditions on the first men, the gods who ruled Egypt as kings, the deeds and death of Osiris, the colonies said to have gone out from Egypt, and the 18,000 years of gods and heroes, on the pages of the 10th-century Vatican manuscript.
