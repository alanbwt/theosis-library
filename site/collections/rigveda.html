Rigveda

c. 1500-1000 BCE (composition); manuscript undated (Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 41)

The complete Rigveda, the oldest collection of Sanskrit hymns, hymn by hymn (1,028 hymns): Aufrecht's accented text, Griffith's English, and the page of a Devanagari manuscript in the University of Pennsylvania collection that carries each verse: a padapāṭha copy of the whole Rigveda, a saṃhitā copy of 1711 for 10.46-10.191, and a copy of 1737 for the Gāyatrī hymn (3.62).

1,028 entries · 2,623 sections, each with its manuscript image, original text and English

  • Images: Vedic seers (c. 1500-1000 BCE); manuscripts 18th-19th c.
  • Original text: Aufrecht (1877), accented e-text via GRETIL
  • English: Ralph T. H. Griffith (1889-1897)
