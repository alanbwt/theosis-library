Pyramid Texts of Unas

c. 2350 BCE (pyramid of Unas, end of the 5th Dynasty)

The Pyramid Texts in the pyramid of Unas at Saqqara (c. 2350 BCE), the oldest Egyptian religious texts: all seventeen inscribed walls, utterance by utterance, with the TLA transliteration and Mercer's 1952 English, each section placed on its wall photograph by column number.

17 entries · 231 sections, each with its manuscript image, original text and English

  • Images: Pyramid of Unas, Saqqara (c. 2350 BCE)
  • Original text: Thesaurus Linguae Aegyptiae, ed. Doris Topmann (2018 data, AED-TEI)
  • English: Samuel A. B. Mercer (1952)
