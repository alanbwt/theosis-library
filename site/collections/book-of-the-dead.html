Book of the Dead (papyrus of Iuefankh, Turin)

Ptolemaic period (332-30 BCE); papyrus from Thebes

The Book of the Dead in the papyrus of Iuefankh (Turin, Ptolemaic period), the roll from which Lepsius took the standard numbering of the spells: spell by spell, with the TLA transliteration, Samuel Birch's 1867 English made from the same papyrus, and the Museo Egizio photographs of the whole roll.
