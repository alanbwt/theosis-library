Roman witnesses to Christ and the Christians

c. 115-120 AD (composition); 11th c. (Laur. Plut. 68.2)

What Roman historians of the early 2nd century wrote about Christ and the first Christians: Tacitus on Nero's punishment of the Christians and the execution of Christus under Pontius Pilate (Annals 15.44), and Suetonius on Claudius expelling the Jews over 'Chrestus' (Claudius 25.4) and on Nero's punishment of the Christians (Nero 16.2), each shown on the 11th-century manuscript page that carries it.

  • Original text: Fisher 1906 (Tacitus) and Ihm 1908 (Suetonius), via Perseus
  • English: Church and Brodribb (1876); J. C. Rolfe (1914)
