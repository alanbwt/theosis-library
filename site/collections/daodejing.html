Daodejing (Mawangdui silk manuscripts)

4th c. BCE (composition); silk copies before 168 BCE (Mawangdui Tomb 3)

The Daodejing chapter by chapter on the two Mawangdui silk manuscripts (copied before 168 BCE), the oldest nearly complete copies, with the column-by-column transcription, the received Wang Bi text and James Legge's English.

81 entries · 162 sections, each with its manuscript image, original text and English

  • Original text: Changsha Mawangdui Han mu jianbo jicheng (2014) column numbers; Wikisource transcription; Wang Bi received text
  • English: James Legge (1891)
