Principal Upanishads

c. 700-200 BCE (composition); 18th or 19th c. (Penn manuscript)

Principal Upanishads in Sanskrit and English, each passage mapped to its page in Sanskrit manuscripts at the University of Pennsylvania: the Isa, the Katha (six vallis), the Prasna (six questions) and the Mandukya from Ms. Coll. 390 Item 869, the Aitareya from Item 1216, and the complete Chandogya in eight chapters from Item 945 (chapter 6, 'thou art that', from Item 1213, copied in 1836).

  • English: F. Max Müller (1879, 1884); R. E. Hume (1921) for the Mandukya
