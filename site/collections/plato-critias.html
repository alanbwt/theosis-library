Plato, Critias (Paris gr. 1807)

c. 360 BCE (composition); c. 850-875 AD (Paris gr. 1807)

The Critias, Plato's unfinished account of ancient Athens and the island of Atlantis, complete as far as Plato wrote it. Every Stephanus section is shown on the page of the oldest medieval manuscript that carries it, Paris gr. 1807 (9th century), with Burnet's Greek and R. G. Bury's English.
