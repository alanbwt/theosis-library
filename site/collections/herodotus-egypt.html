Herodotus on the kings of Egypt

c. 430 BCE (Herodotus); 10th c. (Laur. Plut. 70.3)

Herodotus' account of the kings of Egypt from Min to Sethos, as the priests of Memphis told it, and their chronology of 341 generations and 11,340 years, on the pages of the oldest manuscript of the Histories (Florence, Laur. Plut. 70.3, 10th c.).
