Berossus, History of Babylonia

c. 290-278 BCE (Berossus); 11th c. (Paris gr. 1711)

The Babylonian priest Berossus' Greek history of his land (c. 290 BCE): Oannes the fish-man who taught civilization, the creation, the ten kings before the flood and the flood of Xisouthros, and the last kings of Babylon, on the pages of a Paris manuscript of George Syncellus and a Florence manuscript of Josephus.
