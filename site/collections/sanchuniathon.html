Sanchuniathon, Phoenician History

c. 100 AD (Philo of Byblos); c. 313 AD (Eusebius); 914 AD (Paris gr. 451)

The Phoenician account of the beginning of the world, the first inventors, and the reigns of Uranus, Kronos (El) and Astarte, from Philo of Byblos' Greek translation of Sanchuniathon, on the pages of the Arethas codex of Eusebius written in 914 AD.
