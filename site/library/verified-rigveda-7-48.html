Introduction

        
Rigveda 7.48 is a hymn of 4 verses addressed to the Ribhus. It stands in book (maṇḍala) 7, the family book of Vasiṣṭha. The Rigveda is the oldest collection of Sanskrit hymns, composed in the second half of the second millennium BCE and handed down orally for many centuries before it was written down.
The manuscript shown is Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 41 (University of Pennsylvania), an undated paper manuscript of the padapāṭha (the word-by-word recitation text, in which every word is written separately and compounds are split) of the whole Ṛgveda in Devanāgarī, 884 leaves in fascicles, 7-9 lines to the page, accents in red; this hymn stands on fol. 524v-525r. Each section below gives the verses that end on one page; the page boundaries were read on the images (the opening words of every page), not counted out. Where the manuscript is a padapāṭha (the word-by-word recitation text), the section shows Aufrecht's continuous (saṃhitā) text first and, as the edition text, the padapāṭha of the same verses, which is the form written on the page.

2ṛ̱bhur ṛ̱bhubhi̍r a̱bhi va̍ḥ syāma̱ vibhvo̍ vi̱bhubhi̱ḥ śava̍sā̱ śavā̍ṁsi | vājo̍ a̱smām̐ a̍vatu̱ vāja̍sātā̱v indre̍ṇa yu̱jā ta̍ruṣema vṛ̱tram ||

English · Ralph T. H. Griffith (1889-1897)

                    
1YE liberal Heroes, Vâjas and R̥ibhukshans, come and delight you with our flowing Soma. May your strength, Vibhûs, as ye come to meet us, turn hitherward your car that brings men profit.
2May we as R̥ibhu with your R̥ibhus conquer strength with our strength, as Vibhûs with the Vibhûs. May Vâja aid us in the fight for booty, and helped by Indra may we quell the foeman.

English · Ralph T. H. Griffith (1889-1897)

                    
3For they rule many tribes with high dominion, and conquer all their foes in close encounter. May Indra, Vibhvan, Vâja, and R̥ibhukshan destroy by turns the wicked foeman's valour.
4Now, Deities, give us ample room and freedom: be all of you, one-minded, our protection. So let the Vasus grant us strength and vigour. Preserve us evermore, ye Gods, with blessings.
