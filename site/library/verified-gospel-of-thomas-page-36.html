Introduction

        
The Gospel of Thomas is a collection of 114 sayings attributed to Jesus, introduced as the hidden sayings written down by Didymos Judas Thomas. The only complete copy is the second tractate of Nag Hammadi Codex II, a papyrus book in Coptic copied in the 4th century and found near Nag Hammadi in Upper Egypt in 1945; it is now in the Coptic Museum, Cairo (inv. 10544). Greek fragments of the work survive in three Oxyrhynchus papyri (P. Oxy. 1, 654 and 655), which are not shown here.
This entry covers the sayings 17-21, which begin on codex page 36; the end of saying 21 is on page 37. The Coptic is given line by line; the small numbers are codex page and line (for example 33.5), so each line can be found on the photograph. Page and line breaks follow the Coptic SCRIPTORIUM edition by Paul Dilley, which he made from the photographs of The Facsimile Edition of the Nag Hammadi Codices, the negatives shown here.
Photograph: Codex II page 36, A-Series negative by Basile Psiroukis, Facsimile Edition p. 46, CCDL nha 2899; Nag Hammadi Archive of the Institute for Antiquity and Christianity, Claremont Colleges Digital Library. Checked on the photograph: page 36 line 1 begins 'ϩⲛ̄ⲟⲩⲏⲉⲓ ⲟⲩⲛ̄ϣⲟⲙⲧ ⲛⲁϣⲱⲡⲉ ⲉϫⲛ̄' and line 35 reads 'ⲧⲏⲥ ⲉⲓⲛⲉ ⲛ̄ⲛⲓⲙ⳿ ⲡⲉϫⲁϥ⳿ ϫⲉⲉⲩⲉⲓⲛⲉ' (the last line stands just above the lower break).

English · Mark M. Mattison

            
Mary said to Jesus, "Whom are your disciples like?"
He said, "They're like little children living in a field which isn't theirs. When the owners of the field come, they'll say, 'Give our field back to us.' They'll strip naked in front of them to let them have it and give them their field.
"So I say that if the owner of the house realizes the bandit is coming, they'll watch out beforehand and won't let the bandit break into the house of their domain and steal their possessions. You, then, watch out for the world! Prepare to defend yourself so that the bandits don't attack you, because what you're expecting will come. May there be a wise person among you!
"When the fruit ripened, the reaper came quickly, sickle in hand, and harvested it. Anyone who has ears to hear should hear!"
