Introduction

        
The Instructions of Shuruppak is a collection of advice that the wise man Shuruppak, son of Ubara-Tutu, gives to his son Ziusudra, the hero who later survives the Flood. It mixes practical counsel (do not buy a donkey that brays, do not put a field beside a road, do not vouch for another man) with moral teaching on speech, theft, violence, family and the gods. It is one of the oldest works of literature: copies of an early version survive from Abu Salabikh and Adab from about 2600-2500 BCE. The version shown here is the Old Babylonian one (about 1900-1700 BCE), which scribes copied in school at Nippur, Ur and other cities.
No single photographed tablet preserves the whole text. Each section below is one paragraph of the ETCSL translation, shown with the photographed tablet or tablets that carry those lines and their own transliterations. The block heading names the tablet, the face and the column; the superscript numbers are ETCSL's line numbers.
Coverage. Of the 288 lines of the ETCSL composite, 269 are shown on at least one photographed tablet. The remaining lines are named in the notes of the sections concerned; the English there renders manuscripts without a usable photograph. The Early Dynastic versions from Abu Salabikh and Adab differ from this text and are not included.
Not shown. ETCSL paragraphs 73-75, 272-273 are left out: no photographed tablet carries at least half of their lines.

English · ETCSL (Black, Cunningham, Flückiger-Hawker, Robson, Taylor, Zólyomi)

            
1-13 In those days, in those far remote days, in those nights, in those faraway nights, in those years, in those far remote years, at that time the wise one who knew how to speak in elaborate words lived in the Land; Šuruppag, the wise one, who knew how to speak with elaborate words lived in the Land. Šuruppag gave instructions to his son; Šuruppag, the son of Ubara-Tutu, gave instructions to his son Zi-ud-sura: My son, let me give you instructions: you should pay attention! Zi-ud-sura, let me speak a word to you: you should pay attention! Do not neglect my instructions! Do not transgress the words I speak! The instructions of an old man are precious; you should comply with them!
