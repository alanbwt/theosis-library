Introduction

        
Rigveda 5.28 is a hymn of 6 verses addressed to Agni. It stands in book (maṇḍala) 5, the family book of the Atri clan. The Rigveda is the oldest collection of Sanskrit hymns, composed in the second half of the second millennium BCE and handed down orally for many centuries before it was written down.
The manuscript shown is Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 41 (University of Pennsylvania), an undated paper manuscript of the padapāṭha (the word-by-word recitation text, in which every word is written separately and compounds are split) of the whole Ṛgveda in Devanāgarī, 884 leaves in fascicles, 7-9 lines to the page, accents in red; this hymn stands on fol. 338v-339r. Each section below gives the verses that end on one page; the page boundaries were read on the images (the opening words of every page), not counted out. Where the manuscript is a padapāṭha (the word-by-word recitation text), the section shows Aufrecht's continuous (saṃhitā) text first and, as the edition text, the padapāṭha of the same verses, which is the form written on the page.

English · Ralph T. H. Griffith (1889-1897)

                    
1AGNI inflamed hath sent to heaven his lustre: he shines forth widely turning unto Morning. Eastward the ladle goes that brings all blessing, praising the Godswith homage and oblation.
2Enkindled, thou art King of the immortal world: him who brings offerings thou attendest for his weal. He whom thou urgest on makes all possessions his: he sets before thee, Agni, gifts that guests may claim.
3Show thyself strong for mighty bliss, O Agni, most excellent be thine effulgent splendours. Make easy to maintain our household lordship, and overcome the might of those who hate us.
4Thy glory, Agni, I adore, kindled, exalted in thy strength. A Steer of brilliant splendour, thou art lighted well at sacred rites.

          

        

        
        


          

            §5-6 · RV 5.28.5-6 (aṣṭaka 4, adhyāya 1, varga 22); Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 41 fol. 339r
            

              
              
              
            

          


          
          

            
Manuscript

            
            
            
            
            
            
Where on the image
Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 41, fol. 339r (numbered 12r by the scribe) (OPenn image 339, lower leaf). Checked on the image: the Rigveda text on this page begins in RV 5.28.5 ‹yakṣi su-adhvara tvam hi›. The next page with Rigveda text, fol. 339v (numbered 12v by the scribe), begins in RV 5.29.3 ‹ahan ahim papi-vān indraḥ›. Verses 5.28.5-6 end on this page; 5.28.5 begins on fol. 338v (numbered 11v by the scribe).

6ā | juhota | duvasyata | agnim | pra-yati | adhvare | vṛṇīdhvam | havya-vāhanam

            

            
          


          

            
English · Ralph T. H. Griffith (1889-1897)

                    
5Agni, invoked and kindled, serve the Gods, thou skilled in sacrifice: For thou art bearer of our gifts.
6Invoke and worship Agni while the sacrificial rite proceeds: For offering-bearer choose ye him.
