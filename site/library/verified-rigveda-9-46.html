Introduction

        
Rigveda 9.46 is a hymn of 6 verses addressed to Soma. It stands in book (maṇḍala) 9, the hymns to Soma Pavamāna. The Rigveda is the oldest collection of Sanskrit hymns, composed in the second half of the second millennium BCE and handed down orally for many centuries before it was written down.
The manuscript shown is Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 41 (University of Pennsylvania), an undated paper manuscript of the padapāṭha (the word-by-word recitation text, in which every word is written separately and compounds are split) of the whole Ṛgveda in Devanāgarī, 884 leaves in fascicles, 7-9 lines to the page, accents in red; this hymn stands on fol. 685r-685v. Each section below gives the verses that end on one page; the page boundaries were read on the images (the opening words of every page), not counted out. Where the manuscript is a padapāṭha (the word-by-word recitation text), the section shows Aufrecht's continuous (saṃhitā) text first and, as the edition text, the padapāṭha of the same verses, which is the form written on the page.

Manuscript

            
            
            
            
Where on the image
Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 41, fol. 685r (numbered 2r by the scribe) (OPenn image 685, lower leaf). Checked on the image: the Rigveda text on this page begins in RV 9.44.6 ‹vasuttaye kratu-vit gātuvit-tamaḥ vājam›. The next page with Rigveda text, fol. 685v (numbered 2v by the scribe), begins in RV 9.46.3 ‹karma-bhiḥ›. Verses 9.46.1-2 end on this page.

          

          

          

            
Vedic Sanskrit · Aufrecht (accented)

                    
1asṛ̍gran de̱vavī̍ta̱ye 'tyā̍sa̱ḥ kṛtvyā̍ iva | kṣara̍ntaḥ parvatā̱vṛdha̍ḥ ||
2pari̍ṣkṛtāsa̱ inda̍vo̱ yoṣe̍va̱ pitryā̍vatī | vā̱yuṁ somā̍ asṛkṣata ||

            
            

              Compare: Padapāṭha (word-by-word text written in the manuscript; Sansknet e-text via GRETIL)
              
        
1asṛgran | deva-vītaye | atyāsaḥ | kṛtvyāḥ-iva | kṣarantaḥ | parvata-vṛdhaḥ
2pari-kṛtāsaḥ | indavaḥ | yoṣā-iva | pitrya-vatī | vāyum | somāḥ | asṛkṣata

            

            
          


          

            
English · Ralph T. H. Griffith (1889-1897)

                    
1LIKE able coursers they have been sent forth to be the feast of Gods, joying in mountains, flowing on.
2To Vâyu flow the Soma-streams, the drops of juice made beautiful Like a bride dowered by her sire.

          

        

        
        


          

            §3-6 · RV 9.46.3-6 (aṣṭaka 7, adhyāya 1, varga 3); Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 41 fol. 685v
            

              
              
              
            

          


          
          

            
Manuscript

            
            
            
            
            
            
Where on the image
Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 41, fol. 685v (numbered 2v by the scribe) (OPenn image 686, upper leaf). Checked on the image: the Rigveda text on this page begins in RV 9.46.3 ‹karma-bhiḥ›. The next page with Rigveda text, fol. 686r (numbered 3r by the scribe), begins in RV 9.48.3 ‹rayim abhi rājānam sukrato›. Vedic Sanskrit · Aufrecht (accented)

                    
3e̱te somā̍sa̱ inda̍va̱ḥ praya̍svantaś ca̱mū su̱tāḥ | indra̍ṁ vardhanti̱ karma̍bhiḥ ||
4ā dhā̍vatā suhastyaḥ śu̱krā gṛ̍bhṇīta ma̱nthinā̍ | gobhi̍ḥ śrīṇīta matsa̱ram ||
5sa pa̍vasva dhanaṁjaya praya̱ntā rādha̍so ma̱haḥ | a̱smabhya̍ṁ soma gātu̱vit ||
6e̱tam mṛ̍janti̱ marjya̱m pava̍māna̱ṁ daśa̱ kṣipa̍ḥ | indrā̍ya matsa̱ram mada̍m ||

            
            

              Compare: Padapāṭha (word-by-word text written in the manuscript; Sansknet e-text via GRETIL)
              
        
3ete | somāsaḥ | indavaḥ | prayasvantaḥ | camū iti | sutāḥ | indram | vardhanti | karma-bhiḥ
4ā | dhāvata | su-hastyaḥ | śukrā | gṛbhṇīta | manthinā | gobhiḥ | śrīṇīta | matsaram
5saḥ | pavasva | dhanam-jaya | pra-yantā | rādhasaḥ | mahaḥ | asmabhyam | soma | gātu-vit
6etam | mṛjanti | marjyam | pavamānam | daśa | kṣipaḥ | indrāya | matsaram | madam

            

            
          


          

            
English · Ralph T. H. Griffith (1889-1897)

                    
3Pressed in the mortar, these, the drops of juice, the Somas rich in food, Give strength to Indra with their work.
4Deft-handed men, run hither, seize the brilliant juices blent with meal, And cook with milk the gladdening draught.
5Thus, Soma, Conqueror of wealth! flow, finding furtherance for us, Giver oF ample opulence.
6This Pavamâna, meet to be adorned, the fingers ten adorn, The draught that shall make Indra glad.
