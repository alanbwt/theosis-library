Introduction

        
Rigveda 1.90 is a hymn of 9 verses addressed to all the gods. It stands in book (maṇḍala) 1, a collection of hymns by many families of poets. The Rigveda is the oldest collection of Sanskrit hymns, composed in the second half of the second millennium BCE and handed down orally for many centuries before it was written down.
The manuscript shown is Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 41 (University of Pennsylvania), an undated paper manuscript of the padapāṭha (the word-by-word recitation text, in which every word is written separately and compounds are split) of the whole Ṛgveda in Devanāgarī, 884 leaves in fascicles, 7-9 lines to the page, accents in red; this hymn stands on fol. 61r-61v. Each section below gives the verses that end on one page; the page boundaries were read on the images (the opening words of every page), not counted out. Where the manuscript is a padapāṭha (the word-by-word recitation text), the section shows Aufrecht's continuous (saṃhitā) text first and, as the edition text, the padapāṭha of the same verses, which is the form written on the page.

English · Ralph T. H. Griffith (1889-1897)

                    
1MAY Varuṇa with guidance straight, and Mitra lead us, he who knows, And Aryaman in accord with Gods.
2For they are dealers forth of wealth, and, not deluded, with their might Guard evermore the holy laws.
3Shelter may they vouchsafe to us, Immortal Gods to mortal men, Chasing our enemies away.
4May they mark out our paths to bliss, Indra, the Maruts, Pûshan, and Bhaga, the Gods to be adored.

English · Ralph T. H. Griffith (1889-1897)

                    
5Yea, Pûshan, Vishṇu, ye who run your course, enrich our hymns with kine; Bless us with all prosperity.
6The winds waft sweets, the rivers pour sweets for the man who keeps the Law So may the plants be sweet for us.
7Sweet be the night and sweet the dawns, sweet the terrestrial atmosphere; Sweet be our Father Heaven to us.
8May the tall tree be full of sweets for us, and full of sweets the Sun: May our milch-kine be sweet for us.
9Be Mitra gracious unto us, and Varuṇa and Aryaman: Indra, Br̥ihaspati be kind, and Vishṇu of the mighty stride.
