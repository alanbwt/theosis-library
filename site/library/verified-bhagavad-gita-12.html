Introduction

        
The Bhagavad Gita ("Song of the Lord") is a dialogue of 700 verses between the prince Arjuna and Krishna, set on the battlefield of Kurukshetra in book 6 (Bhishmaparvan) of the Mahabharata. Its date of composition is debated; many scholars place it in the last centuries BCE. This is chapter 12, The Yoga of Devotion (20 verses as numbered in the manuscript and in Besant's edition).



The pages shown are from a complete Sanskrit manuscript of the Gita at the University of Pennsylvania (Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 559), 90 paper leaves of 7 lines in Devanagari, with speaker headings, colophons and verse-end marks in red. Its final colophon (fol. 90r) dates the copy to Saṃvat 1851, the third day of the bright half of Caitra (1794 CE). Chapter 12 runs from fol. 61r to the chapter colophon on fol. 63v.



Each section below is one manuscript page: the verses whose text ends on that page, located by reading the scribe's verse numbers and the first and last words on the image. When a verse begins at the foot of one page and ends on the next, both pages are shown. The Sanskrit and English are from Annie Besant's edition and translation (4th edition, 1922), which prints each verse in Devanagari with its English; the manuscript's spelling differs from the printed text in small details.

            §1-2 · Bhagavad Gita 12.1-2; Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 559, fol. 61r
            

              
              
              
            

          


          
          

            
Manuscript

            
            
            
            
Where on the image
fol. 61r, OPenn image 6555_0060 (lower leaf). Checked on the image: ||55|| line 2; colophon lines 2-4 '... viśvarūpadarśanaṃ nāmaikādaśo 'dhyāyaḥ ||11||'; arjuna uvāca line 4; 12.1 ends line 5 (||1||), 12.2 ends line 7 (||2||), 12.3 begins line 7.

          

          

          

            
Sanskrit · Devanagari (Besant 1922)

            
1अर्जुन उवाच ।
एवं सततयुक्ता ये भक्तास्त्वां पर्युपासते ।
ये चाप्यक्षरमव्यक्तं तेषां के योगवित्तमाः ॥ १ ॥
2श्रीभगवानुवाच ।
मय्यावेश्य मनो ये मां नित्ययुक्ता उपासते ।
श्रद्धया परयोपेतास्ते मे युक्ततमा मताः ॥ २ ॥

            
          


          

            
English · Annie Besant (1922)

            
1Arjuna said:
Those devotees who ever harmonised worship Thee, and those also who worship the Indestructible, the Unmanifested, whether of these is the more learned in yoga?
2The Blessed Lord said:
They who with mind fixed on Me ever harmonised worship Me, with faith supreme endowed, these, in My opinion, are best in yoga.

          

        

        
        


          

            §3-6 · Bhagavad Gita 12.3-6; Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 559, fol. 61r and fol. 61v

Sanskrit · Devanagari (Besant 1922)

            
3ये त्वक्षरमनिर्देश्यमव्यक्तं पर्युपासते ।
सर्वत्रगमचिन्त्यं च कूटस्थमचलं ध्रुवम् ॥ ३ ॥
4संनियम्येन्द्रियग्रामं सर्वत्र समबुद्धयः ।
ते प्राप्नुवन्ति मामेव सर्वभूतहिते रताः ॥ ४ ॥
5क्लेशोऽधिकतरस्तेषामव्यक्तासक्तचेतसाम् ।
अव्यक्ता हि गतिर्दुःखं देहवद्भिरवाप्यते ॥ ५ ॥
6ये तु सर्वाणि कर्माणि मयि संन्यस्य मत्पराः ।
अनन्येनैव योगेन मां ध्यायन्त उपासते ॥ ६ ॥

            
          


          

            
English · Annie Besant (1922)

            
3They who worship the Indestructible, the Ineffable, the unmanifested, Omnipresent, and Unthinkable, the Unchanging, Immutable, Eternal,
4Restraining and subduing the senses, regarding everything equally, in the welfare of all rejoicing, these also come unto Me.
5The difficulty of those whose minds are set on the Unmanifested is greater; for the path of the Unmanifested is hard for the embodied to reach.
6Those verily who, renouncing all actions in Me and intent on Me, worship meditating on Me, with whole-hearted yoga,

          

        

        
        


          

            §7-11 · Bhagavad Gita 12.7-11; Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 559, fol. 61v and fol. 62r
            

              
              
              
            

          


          
          

            
Manuscript

            
            
            
            
            
            
Where on the image
fol. 62r, OPenn image 6555_0061 (lower leaf). Checked on the image: ||7|| line 1 ... ||11|| end of line 7. Verse 12.7 begins on the previous page, fol. 61v, so both pages are shown.

          

          

          

            
Sanskrit · Devanagari (Besant 1922)

            
7तेषामहं समुद्धर्ता मृत्युसंसारसागरात् ।
भवामि न चिरात्पार्थ मय्यावेशितचेतसाम् ॥ ७ ॥
8मय्येव मन आधत्स्व मयि बुद्धिं निवेशय ।
निवसिष्यसि मय्येव अत ऊर्ध्वं न संशयः ॥ ८ ॥
9अथ चित्तं समाधातुं न शक्नोषि मयि स्थिरम् ।
अभ्यासयोगेन ततो मामिच्छाप्तुं धनञ्जय ॥ ९ ॥
10अभ्यासेऽप्यसमर्थोऽसि मत्कर्मपरमो भव ।
मदर्थमपि कर्माणि कुर्वन्सिद्धिमवाप्स्यसि ॥ १० ॥
11अथैतदप्यशक्तोऽसि कर्तुं मद्योगमाश्रितः ।
सर्वकर्मफलत्यागं ततः कुरु यतात्मवान् ॥ ११ ॥

            
          


          

            
English · Annie Besant (1922)

            
7These I speedily lift up from the ocean of death and existence, O Pârtha, their minds being fixed on Me.
8Place thy mind in me, into Me let thy Reason enter; then without doubt thou shalt abide in Me hereafter.
9And if thou art not able firmly to fix thy mind on Me, then by the yoga of practice seek to reach Me, O Dhananjaya.
10If also thou art not equal to constant practice, be intent on My service; performing actions for My sake, thou shalt attain perfection.
11If even to do this thou hast not strength, then, taking refuge in union with Me, renounce all fruit of action with the self controlled.

          

        

        
        


          

            §12-15 · Bhagavad Gita 12.12-15; Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 559, fol. 62v
            

              
              
              
            

          


          
          

            
Manuscript

            
            
            
            
Where on the image
fol. 62v, OPenn image 6555_0062 (upper leaf). Checked on the image: ||12|| line 2 ... ||15|| line 6.

          

          

          

            
Sanskrit · Devanagari (Besant 1922)

            
12श्रेयो हि ज्ञानमभ्यासाज्ज्ञानाद्ध्यानं विशिष्यते ।
ध्यानात्कर्मफलत्यागस्त्यागाच्छान्तिरनन्तरम् ॥ १२ ॥
13अद्वेष्टा सर्वभूतानां मैत्रः करुण एव च ।
निर्ममो निरहङ्कारः समदुःखसुखः क्षमी ॥ १३ ॥
14सन्तुष्टः सततं योगी यतात्मा दृढनिश्चयः ।
मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्यो मे भक्तः स मे प्रियः ॥ १४ ॥
15यस्मान्नोद्विजते लोको लोकान्नोद्विजते च यः ।
हर्षामर्षभयोद्वेगैर्मुक्तो यः स च मे प्रियः ॥ १५ ॥

            
          


          

            
English · Annie Besant (1922)

            
12Better indeed is wisdom than constant-practice; than wisdom, meditation is better; than meditation, renunciation of the fruit of action; on renunciation follows peace.
13He who beareth no ill-will to any being, friendly and compassionate, without attachment and egoism, balanced in pleasure and pain, and forgiving,
14Ever content, harmonious, with the self-controlled, resolute, with mind and Reason dedicated to Me, he, My devotee, is dear to Me.
15He from whom the world doth not shrink away, who doth not shrink away from the world, freed from the anxieties of joy, anger, and fear, he is dear to me.

          

        

        
        


          

            §16-19 · Bhagavad Gita 12.16-19; Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 559, fol. 62v and fol. 63r

Sanskrit · Devanagari (Besant 1922)

            
16अनपेक्षः शुचिर्दक्ष उदासीनो गतव्यथः ।
सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः ॥ १६ ॥
17यो न हृष्यति न द्वेष्टि न शोचति न काङ्क्षति ।
शुभाशुभपरित्यागी भक्तिमान्यः स मे प्रियः ॥ १७ ॥
18समः शत्रौ च मित्रे च तथा मानपमानयोः ।
शीतोष्णसुखदुःखेषु समः सङ्गविवर्जितः ॥ १८ ॥
19तुल्यनिन्दास्तुतिर्मौनी सन्तुष्टो येनकेनचित् ।
अनिकेतः स्थिरमतिर्भक्तिमान्मे प्रियो नरः ॥ १९ ॥

            
          


          

            
English · Annie Besant (1922)

            
16He who wants nothing, is pure, expert, passionless, untroubled, renouncing every undertaking, he, My devotee, is dear to Me.
17He who neither loveth nor hateth, nor grieveth, nor desireth, renouncing good and evil, full of devotion, he is dear to Me.
18Alike to foe and friend, and also in fame and ignominy, alike in cold and heat, pleasures and pains, destitute of attachment,
19Taking equally praise and reproach, silent, wholly content with what cometh, homeless, firm in mind, full of devotion, that man is dear to Me.

Notes

        
    
          
          
  1. 
            Sanskrit and English: Annie Besant, The Bhagavad-Gita, 4th edition (Madras: G. A. Natesan, 1922), transcription validated on English Wikisource. Besant's footnotes are not reproduced.

    4.
