Introduction

        
This clay tablet (IM 055591, National Museum of Iraq, Baghdad, Iraq) is a proto-cuneiform document of the Uruk III period, about 3200-3000 BCE, from Jemdet Nasr (CDLI catalogues the provenience as uncertain, attributed to Jemdet Nasr). It is a record of the length, width and area of the fields of officials (the chief exchange agent, the equid herder, the chief justice and others), with totals on the reverse, including the field of the lord.
Proto-cuneiform is the earliest stage of cuneiform writing, used for bookkeeping in southern Mesopotamia. It records numbers in several counting systems (shown in the transliteration as signs N01, N14, N34 and so on) together with signs for goods, offices and places. It does not yet write full sentences, so the English is an interpretation of the entries rather than a translation of connected language.
The sections follow the faces and columns of the tablet. The transliteration and the English are CDLI's, line by line with the same line numbers.

Notes

        
    
          
          
  1. 
            Transliteration: CDLI ATF (proto-cuneiform). Numerals are written as counts of numeral signs, e.g. 4(N14) = four N14 signs; ~a, ~b mark sign variants; [ ] broken, # damaged; sz is shown as š.
          
    2. 
          
          
  2. 
            English: CDLI's line-by-line translation (#tr.en lines of the ATF).
          
    3. 
          
          
  3. 
            CDLI notes: obverse, line 1.b2: calculation: 320 × 42 = 13,440 sar, = 7 bur3 + 8 4/10 iku; obverse, line 2.b2: calculation: 320 × 45 = 14,400 sar, = 6 bur3 + 2 bur3; obverse, line 3.b2: calculation: 354 × 37 = 13,098 sar, = 7 bur3 + 4 98/100 iku; obverse, line 4.b2: calculation: 354 × 37 = 13,098 sar, = 7 bur3 + 4 98/100 iku; obverse, line 5.b2: calculation: 354 × 37 = 13,098 sar, = 7 bur3 + 4 98/100 iku; reverse, column 1, line 1: calculation in bur3: 30 × 2 = 60; reverse, column 1, line 2: calculation in bur3: 7 + 6 + 7 + 7 + 3 = 30; reverse, column 1, line 3: calculation in iku: 8 4/10 + 36 + 5 + 5 + 1 7/10 = 56 1/10 (text has 38 1/10, leaving out 2 bur3 of 2.d.); reverse, column 2, line 1: calculation: 1 šar2 + 30 bur3 + 2 bur3 2 1/10 iku = 1 šar2 32 bur3 2 1/10 iku
