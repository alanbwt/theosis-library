It gives the letters as the first scribe wrote them: no accents or breathings, the scribe's own spelling, and the sacred names in their contracted form (θς for θεος, ις for ιησους, κς for κυριος; the overline is not reproduced). Corrections by later hands are not shown. Letters in square brackets are not legible on the page and were restored by the transcribers, a dot under a letter marks an uncertain reading, and [...] marks text lost to damage. English · King James Version (1611)

                    
1Then Jesus six days before the passover came to Bethany, where Lazarus was which had been dead, whom he raised from the dead. 2There they made him a supper; and Martha served: but Lazarus was one of them that sat at the table with him. 3Then took Mary a pound of ointment of spikenard, very costly, and anointed the feet of Jesus, and wiped his feet with her hair: and the house was filled with the odour of the ointment.
