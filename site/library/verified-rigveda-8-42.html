Introduction

        
Rigveda 8.42 is a hymn of 6 verses addressed to Varuna and the Asvins. It stands in book (maṇḍala) 8, the book of the Kaṇva and Aṅgiras poets. The Rigveda is the oldest collection of Sanskrit hymns, composed in the second half of the second millennium BCE and handed down orally for many centuries before it was written down.
The manuscript shown is Penn Ms. Coll. 390 Item 41 (University of Pennsylvania), an undated paper manuscript of the padapāṭha (the word-by-word recitation text, in which every word is written separately and compounds are split) of the whole Ṛgveda in Devanāgarī, 884 leaves in fascicles, 7-9 lines to the page, accents in red; this hymn stands on fol. 613r-613v. Each section below gives the verses that end on one page; the page boundaries were read on the images (the opening words of every page), not counted out. Where the manuscript is a padapāṭha (the word-by-word recitation text), the section shows Aufrecht's continuous (saṃhitā) text first and, as the edition text, the padapāṭha of the same verses, which is the form written on the page.

English · Ralph T. H. Griffith (1889-1897)

                    
1LORD of all wealth, the Asura propped the heavens, and measured out the broad earth's wide expanses. He, King supreme, approached all living creatures. All these are Varuṇa's holy operations.
2So humbly worship Varuṇa the Mighty revere the wise Guard of World Immortal. May he vouchsafe us triply-barred protection. O Earth and Heaven, within your lap preserve us.
3Sharpen this song of him who strives his utmost, sharpen, God Varuṇa, his strength and insight; May we ascend the ship that bears us safely, whereby we may pass over all misfortune.
4Aṣvins, with songs the singer stones have made you hasten hitherward, Nâsatyas, to the Soma-draught. Let all the others die away.
5As the sage Atri with his hymns, O Aṣvins, called you eagerly, Nâsatyas, to the Soma-draught. Let all the others die away.
6So have I called you to our aid, even as the wise have called of old, Nâsatyas, to the Soma-draught. Let all the others die away.
