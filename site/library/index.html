Library

2,363 entries in 12 collections, oldest civilisations first. Every section shows the original document, the original language and an English translation.

Mesopotamia · Old Testament · Greece · Dead Sea Scrolls & Second Temple · New Testament · Later Traditions

Mesopotamia

Sumerian and Babylonian literature, law and history on clay.

Epic of Gilgamesh (Nineveh tablets)
c. 1200 BCE (composition); 7th c. BCE tablets · 19 entries
The Standard Babylonian epic on the clay tablets from Ashurbanipal's library at Nineveh.
Old Testament

The Hebrew scriptures in their oldest complete manuscript.

Leningrad Codex
1008 AD · 929 entries
The oldest complete manuscript of the Hebrew Old Testament, all 929 chapters on their pages.
Greece

Homer, Hesiod, Plato and the Greek historians.

Homer, Iliad (Venetus A)
c. 750 BCE (composition); 10th c. manuscript · 24 entries
All 24 books of the Iliad on the Venetus A manuscript, line by line from the Homer Multitext project.
Dead Sea Scrolls & Second Temple

Jewish writings from the Babylonian exile to the fall of the Temple.

Great Isaiah Scroll (1QIsa-a)
c. 125 BCE · 66 entries
The complete book of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 1, a thousand years older than the medieval codices, column by column.
Josephus (Vatican manuscripts)
c. 93/94 AD (composition); 10th-14th c. (Vatican manuscripts) · 1 entry
Josephus on Jesus, on three Vatican manuscripts.
New Testament

The great codices of the Greek Bible, chapter by chapter.

Codex Vaticanus
c. 300-350 AD · 220 entries
The Vatican Library's 4th-century Greek Bible, New Testament chapters mapped verse by verse to its pages.
Codex Sinaiticus
c. 330-360 AD · 699 entries
The oldest complete New Testament, with much of the Greek Old Testament, the Epistle of Barnabas and the Shepherd of Hermas. Every chapter on its own folio from the Codex Sinaiticus Project.
Codex Bezae
c. 400 AD · 45 entries
The Greek-Latin Gospels and Acts at Cambridge, with its distinctive Western text.
Codex Washingtonianus
4th-5th c. AD · 88 entries
The Freer Gospels in the Smithsonian, including the Freer Logion in Mark 16.
Codex Alexandrinus
c. 400-440 AD · 228 entries
The British Library's 5th-century Greek Bible, New Testament chapters on the 1909 photographic facsimile.
Later Traditions

Later scriptures and poetry kept for comparison.

Quran, Codex Parisino-petropolitanus
late 7th c. AD · 33 entries
One of the oldest Quran manuscripts, in early Hijazi script, suras mapped to folios.
Poetic Edda (Codex Regius)
c. 1270 AD · 11 entries
The mythological poems of the Codex Regius, from the creation of the world to Ragnarok.
