Introduction

        
Book 7 of the Iliad (482 lines in Monro and Allen's edition) as written in Venetus A (Venice, Biblioteca Nazionale Marciana, Marc. gr. Z. 454 = 822), the oldest complete manuscript of the Iliad, copied in the 10th century with the text in the centre of each page and the scholia around it. This book is on fol. 91r-100v; each section below is one page of the manuscript and links that page.
The page of every line comes from the Homer Multitext Project's index of the manuscript (release 2024c), which records where each line stands on the page photographs.
Greek: the manuscript's own text, from the Homer Multitext diplomatic edition of Venetus A, with its accents, punctuation and signs as given there. The Oxford text of Monro and Allen (1920) is given for comparison.
English: A. T. Murray's translation (Loeb Classical Library, 1924). Murray numbers every fifth line and starts a new paragraph at many other lines; the English is divided only at those points, and a passage that runs across a page break goes with the page that holds most of its lines. The English of a section can therefore begin or end a few lines away from the Greek; each section gives its exact English line range.
Not in Venetus A: lines 7.368-369 of the standard text are not in this manuscript. They are shown in the Monro and Allen text and in the English of the page where they would stand.
