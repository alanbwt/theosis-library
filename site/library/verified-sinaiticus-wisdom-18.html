            §18.1-8 · Wisdom of Solomon 18:1-8
            

              
              
              
            

          


          
          

            
Manuscript

            
            
            
          

          

          

            
Greek · Codex Sinaiticus transcription

                    
1τοιϲ δε οϲιοιϲ ϲου . μεγιϲτον ην φωϲ ων φωνην μεν ακουοντεϲ · μορφην δε ουχʼ ορωντεϲ οτι μεν ουν κακεινοι επεπονθιϲαν · εμακαριζον 2οτι δ ου βλαϲτουϲιν · προηδικημενοι · ευχαριϲτουϲιν και του διενεχθηναι · χαριν οδοντων 3ανθ ων πυριφλεγη ϲτυλον ολιγον μεν · αγνωϲτου οδοιποριαϲ ηλιον δε αβλαβη φιλοτιμιαϲ παρεϲχε 4αξιοι μεν γαρ εκεινοι ϲτερηθηναι φωτοϲ · και φυλακιϲθηναι ϲκοτουϲ οι κατακλιϲτουϲ φυλαϲϲοντεϲ . τουϲ υϊουϲ ϲου δι ων εμελλεν το αφθαρτον νομου φωτοϲ · τω αιωνι διδοϲθαι · 5βουλευϲαμενουϲ αυτουϲ · τα των οϲιων αποκτινειν νηπια και ενοϲ εκτεθεντοϲ τεκνου · και ϲωθεντοϲ ειϲ ελεγχον το αυτων · αφειλω πληθοϲ τεκνων τουϲ δε εχθρουϲ του λαου ομοθυμαδον · απωλεϲαϲ εν ϋδατι ϲφοδρω 6εκινη η νυξ · προεγνωϲθη πατραϲιν ημων ϊνα αϲφαλωϲ ειδοτεϲ · οιϲ επιϲτευϲαν ορκοιϲ · επιθυμηϲωϲιν 7προϲεδεχθη υπο λαου ϲου ϲωτηρια μεν δικαιων · εχθρων δε απωλια · 8ωϲ γαρ ετιμωρηϲω τουϲ υπεναντιουϲ τουτω ημαϲ προϲκαλεϲαμενοϲ . εδοξαϲαϲ

            
          


          

            
English · King James Version Apocrypha (1611; 1769 text)

                    
1Nevertheless thy saints had a very great light, whose voice they hearing, and not seeing their shape, because they also had not suffered the same things, they counted them happy. 2But for that they did not hurt them now, of whom they had been wronged before, they thanked them, and besought them pardon for that they had been enemies. 3Instead whereof thou gavest them a burning pillar of fire, both to be a guide of the unknown journey, and an harmless sun to entertain them honourably. 4For they were worthy to be deprived of light and imprisoned in darkness, who had kept thy sons shut up, by whom the uncorrupt light of the law was to be given unto the world. 5And when they had determined to slay the babes of the saints, one child being cast forth, and saved, to reprove them, thou tookest away the multitude of their children, and destroyedst them altogether in a mighty water. 6Of that night were our fathers certified afore, that assuredly knowing unto what oaths they had given credence, they might afterwards be of good cheer. 7So of thy people was accepted both the salvation of the righteous, and destruction of the enemies. 8For wherewith thou didst punish our adversaries, by the same thou didst glorify us, whom thou hadst called.

          

        

        
        


          

            §18.9-16 · Wisdom of Solomon 18:9-16
            

              
              
              
            

          


          
          

            
Manuscript

            
            
            
          

          

          

            
Greek · Codex Sinaiticus transcription

                    
9κρυφη γαρ εθυϲιαζον οϲιοι παιδεϲ αγαθων και τον τηϲ οϲιοτητοϲ νομον · εν ομονοια διεθεντο · των αυτων ομοιωϲ και αγαθων και κινδυνων · μεταλημψεϲθαι τουϲ αγιουϲ πρων · ηδη προαναμελποντων αινουϲ · 10αντηχει δ αϲυμφωνοϲ εχθρων η βοη και οικτρα διεφερετο φωνη · θρηνουμενων παιδων 11ομοια δε δικη · δουλοϲ αμα δεϲποτη κολαϲθειϲ και δημοτηϲ βαϲιλεια ταυτα παϲχων 12ομοθυμοαδον παντεϲ · εν ενι ονοματι θανατου νεκρουϲ ειχον · αναριθμητουϲ ˙ ουδε γαρ προϲ το θαψαι · οι ζωντεϲ ηϲαν ϊκανοι · επι προϲ μιαν ροπην · εντιμοτερα γενεϲειϲ αυτων διεφθαρτο · 13παντα γαρ απιϲτουντεϲ δια ταϲ φαρμακιαϲ επι τω των πρωτοτοκων ολεθρω · ωμολογηϲαν θυ ϋϊον λαον ειναι · 14ηϲυχου γαρ ϲειγηϲ περιεχουϲηϲ τα παντα και νυκτοϲ · εν ϊδιω ταχει μεϲαζουϲηϲ · 15ο παντοδυναμοϲ ϲου λογοϲ απ ουρανων · εκ θρονων βαϲιλιων · αποτομοϲ πολεμιϲτηϲ · ειϲ μεϲον τηϲ ολεθριαϲ ηλατο γηϲ ξιφοϲ οξυ · την ανϋποκριτν επιταγην ϲου φερων · 16και ϲταϲ · επληρωϲεν τα παντα θανατου και ουρανου μεν ηπτετο . βεβηκει δε γηϲ

            
          


          

            
English · King James Version Apocrypha (1611; 1769 text)

                    
9For the righteous children of good men did sacrifice secretly, and with one consent made a holy law, that the saints should be like partakers of the same good and evil, the fathers now singing out the songs of praise. 10But on the other side there sounded an ill according cry of the enemies, and a lamentable noise was carried abroad for children that were bewailed. 11The master and the servant were punished after one manner; and like as the king, so suffered the common person. 12So they all together had innumerable dead with one kind of death; neither were the living sufficient to bury them: for in one moment the noblest offspring of them was destroyed. 13For whereas they would not believe any thing by reason of the enchantments; upon the destruction of the firstborn, they acknowledged this people to be the sons of God. 14For while all things were in quiet silence, and that night was in the midst of her swift course, 15Thine Almighty word leaped down from heaven out of thy royal throne, as a fierce man of war into the midst of a land of destruction, 16And brought thine unfeigned commandment as a sharp sword, and standing up filled all things with death; and it touched the heaven, but it stood upon the earth.

          

        

        
        


          

            §18.17-24 · Wisdom of Solomon 18:17-24
            

              
              
              
            

          


          
          

            
Manuscript

            
            
            
            
            
          

          

          

            
Greek · Codex Sinaiticus transcription

                    
17τοτε παραχρημα φανταϲιαι μεν ονιρων δεινων · εξεταραξαν αυτουϲ φοβοι δε · επεϲτηϲαν αδοκητοι · 18και αλλοϲ αλλαχη ριφιϲ ημιθνητοϲ δι ην εθνηϲκον αιτιαν · ενεφανιζεν 19οι γαρ ονιροι θορυβηϲαντεϲ αυτουϲ . προεμηνυϲαν ϊνα μη αγνοουντεϲ δι ο κακωϲ παϲχουϲιν · απολωνται 20ηψατο δε και δικαιων τοτε . πιρα θανατου και θραυϲειϲ εν ερημω . εγενετο πληθουϲ αλλ ουκ επι πολυ εμινεν η οργη ϲου 21ϲπευϲαϲ γαρ ανηρ αμεμπτοϲ . προεμαχηϲεν το τηϲ ϊδιαϲ λιτουργιαϲ οπλον · προϲευχην και θυμιαματοϲ · εξιλαϲμον κομιϲαϲ αντεϲτη θυμω . και περαϲ επεθηκεν τη ϲυμφορα δεικνυϲ · οτι ϲοϲ εϲτιν θεραπων · 22ενικηϲεν δε τον οχλον · ουκ ϊϲχυει του ϲωματοϲ · ουχ οπλων ενεργεια αλλα λογω · τον κολαζοντα ϋπεταξεν ορκουϲ πατερων και διαθηκαϲ · ϋπομνηϲαϲ 23ϲωρηδον γαρ ηδη πεπτωκοτων επ αλληλων νεκρων μεταξυ ϲταϲ · ανεκοψεν την οργην και διεϲχιϲεν · την προϲ τουϲ ζωνταϲ οδον 24επι γαρ ποδηρουϲ ενδυματοϲ ην · ολοϲ ο κοϲμοϲ και πρων δοξαι · επι τετραϲτιχου λιθου γλυφη και μεγαλωϲυνη ϲου · επι διαδηματοϲ κεφαληϲ αυτου

            
          


          

            
English · King James Version Apocrypha (1611; 1769 text)

                    
17Then suddenly visions of horrible dreams troubled them sore, and terrors came upon them unlooked for. 18And one thrown here, and another there, half dead, shewed the cause of his death. 19For the dreams that troubled them did foreshew this, lest they should perish, and not know why they were afflicted. 20Yea, the tasting of death touched the righteous also, and there was a destruction of the multitude in the wilderness: but the wrath endured not long. 21For then the blameless man made haste, and stood forth to defend them; and bringing the shield of his proper ministry, even prayer, and the propitiation of incense, set himself against the wrath, and so brought the calamity to an end, declaring that he was thy servant. 22So he overcame the destroyer, not with strength of body, nor force of arms, but with a word subdued him that punished, alleging the oaths and covenants made with the fathers. 23For when the dead were now fallen down by heaps one upon another, standing between, he stayed the wrath, and parted the way to the living. 24For in the long garment was the whole world, and in the four rows of the stones was the glory of the fathers graven, and thy Majesty upon the diadem of his head.
