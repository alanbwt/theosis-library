Introduction

        
The Gospel of Thomas is a collection of 114 sayings attributed to Jesus, introduced as the hidden sayings written down by Didymos Judas Thomas. The only complete copy is the second tractate of Nag Hammadi Codex II, a papyrus book in Coptic copied in the 4th century and found near Nag Hammadi in Upper Egypt in 1945; it is now in the Coptic Museum, Cairo (inv. 10544). Greek fragments of the work survive in three Oxyrhynchus papyri (P. Oxy. 1, 654 and 655), which are not shown here.
This entry covers the sayings 9-13, which begin on codex page 34; the end of saying 13 is on page 35. The Coptic is given line by line; the small numbers are codex page and line (for example 33.5), so each line can be found on the photograph. Page and line breaks follow the Coptic SCRIPTORIUM edition by Paul Dilley, which he made from the photographs of The Facsimile Edition of the Nag Hammadi Codices, the negatives shown here.
Photograph: Codex II page 34, left half of P-Series negative no. 191 of September 1975 ('P II, 34+109'), Facsimile Edition p. 44, CCDL nha 2569; Nag Hammadi Archive of the Institute for Antiquity and Christianity, Claremont Colleges Digital Library. Checked on the photograph: page 34 line 1 begins 'ⲥⲏⲧ̂ ⲉⲑⲁⲗⲁⲥⲥⲁ ⲁϥⲥⲱⲧⲡ · ⲙ̄ⲡⲛⲟϭ ⲛ̄' and line 34 reads 'ⲅⲉⲗⲟⲥ ⲛ̄ⲇⲓⲕⲁⲓⲟⲥ ⲡⲉϫⲁϥ ⲛⲁϥ ⲛ̄ϭⲓ' (shown from the P-Series negative, since the A-Series negative of this page shows only loose fragments; the name Matthew is divided, ⲙⲁⲑ⳿ at the end of line 34 and ⲑⲁⲓⲟⲥ at the start of page 35, where the edition puts the whole name on 35.1).

English · Mark M. Mattison

            
Jesus said to his disciples, "If you were to compare me to someone, who would you say I'm like?"
Simon Peter said to him, "You're like a just angel."
Matthew said to him, "You're like a wise philosopher."
Thomas said to him, "Teacher, I'm completely unable to say whom you're like."
Jesus said, "I'm not your teacher. Because you've drunk, you've become intoxicated by the bubbling spring I've measured out."
He took him aside and told him three things. When Thomas returned to his companions, they asked, "What did Jesus say to you?"
Thomas said to them, "If I tell you one of the things he said to me, you'll pick up stones and cast them at me, and fire will come out of the stones and burn you up."

            The small numbers in the Coptic are codex page and line (page.line) as marked in the edition. Where a word is divided between two lines on the papyrus, the edition sometimes keeps the word whole on one line, so a line can begin a few letters earlier or later on the photograph (for example 39.33-34, and the page change inside saying 13).

  4.
