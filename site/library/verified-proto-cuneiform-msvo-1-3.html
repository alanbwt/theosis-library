Introduction

        
This clay tablet (Ashm 1926-0577, Ashmolean Museum, Oxford, UK) is a proto-cuneiform document of the Uruk III period, about 3200-3000 BCE, from Jemdet Nasr (CDLI catalogues the provenience as uncertain, attributed to Jemdet Nasr). It is a record of the length, width and area of the fields of officials (the wife of the lord, the equid herder, the chief trade agent, the priest and the chief justice), with totals on the reverse.
Proto-cuneiform is the earliest stage of cuneiform writing, used for bookkeeping in southern Mesopotamia. It records numbers in several counting systems (shown in the transliteration as signs N01, N14, N34 and so on) together with signs for goods, offices and places. It does not yet write full sentences, so the English is an interpretation of the entries rather than a translation of connected language.
The sections follow the faces and columns of the tablet. The transliteration and the English are CDLI's, line by line with the same line numbers.

Notes

        
    
          
          
  1. 
            Transliteration: CDLI ATF (proto-cuneiform). Numerals are written as counts of numeral signs, e.g. 4(N14) = four N14 signs; ~a, ~b mark sign variants; [ ] broken, # damaged; sz is shown as š.
          
    2. 
          
          
  2. 
            English: CDLI's line-by-line translation (#tr.en lines of the ATF).
          
    3. 
          
          
  3. 
            CDLI notes: obverse, line 1.b: calculation: 102 × 100 = 10,200 sar, = 5 2/3 bur3; obverse, line 2.b: calculation: 102 × 100 = 10,200 sar, = 5 2/3 bur3; obverse, line 3.b: calculation: 102 × 100 = 10,200 sar, = 5 2/3 bur3; obverse, line 4.b2: calculation: 36 4/10 × 100 = 3640 sar, = 2 bur3 + 4/10 iku; obverse, line 5.b: calculation: 102 × 100 = 10,200 sar, = 5 2/3 bur3; reverse, column 1, line 1: calculation in bur3: 24 2/3 × 2 = 49 1/3; reverse, column 1, line 2: calculation in bur3: 5 2/3 + 5 2/3 + 5 2/3 + 2 + 5 2/3 = 24 2/3; reverse, column 1, line 3: calculation in iku: 4/10 = 4/10; reverse, column 2, line 1: calculation: 49 bur3 1 eše3 + 24 bur3 2 eše3 + 4/10 iku = 1 šar2 14 bur3 4/10 iku
