Reading Path

The Flood before history

The great flood as the oldest written traditions tell it, from the Sumerian king lists to Gilgamesh, Genesis, Enoch and Plato, each on the tablet or manuscript that carries it.

  1. 1
    c. 1800 BCE · Sumerian · Sumerian King List

    Sumerian King List: kings before the Flood

    Kingship descends from heaven to Eridu; eight kings reign for 241,200 years, and then the flood sweeps over.

  2. 2
    c. 1800 BCE · Sumerian · Sumerian King List

    Sumerian King List: after the Flood

    Kingship descends again, this time to Kish, and the reigns grow shorter.

  3. 3
    c. 1600 BCE · Sumerian · Nippur tablet

    Eridu Genesis: seven days and seven nights

    The storms arise together, the flood sweeps the land, and the huge boat is tossed for seven days and nights.

  4. 4
    c. 1600 BCE · Sumerian · Nippur tablet

    Eridu Genesis: Ziusudra granted life

    The king who preserved the seed of mankind is given life like a god.

  5. 5
    7th c. BCE tablets · Akkadian · Gilgamesh XI

    Gilgamesh XI: "Demolish the house, build a boat"

    Uta-napishti tells Gilgamesh how the god Ea warned him of the Flood.

  6. 6
    7th c. BCE tablets · Akkadian · Gilgamesh XI (K.3375, the Flood Tablet)

    Gilgamesh XI: the dove, the swallow and the raven

    The boat grounds on Mount Nimush and the birds are sent out, read on the Flood Tablet itself.

  7. 7
    c. 290 BCE · Greek · Berossus

    Berossus: Xisuthrus builds a vessel

    Kronos warns Xisuthrus in a dream to bury the writings at Sippar and build a vessel for his family and the animals.

  8. 8
    8 AD (11th c. manuscript) · Latin · Ovid, Metamorphoses 1

    Ovid: Deucalion and Pyrrha on Parnassus

    The Greek and Roman flood: the waters cover all but the twin peaks of Parnassus, where a small boat brings the one just man and his wife to land.

  9. 9
    1008 AD manuscript · Hebrew · Genesis 6

    Genesis 6: the sons of God and the giants

    The Nephilim in the earth in those days, and the decision to send the flood.

  10. 10
    3rd c. BCE · Ge'ez · 1 Enoch 10

    1 Enoch 10: Noah is warned

    Uriel is sent to tell Noah to hide himself; the Watchers who fathered the giants are bound.
