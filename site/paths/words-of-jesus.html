Reading Path

What did Jesus say about divinity?

The words attributed to Jesus on the divine nature, followed across Mark, Matthew and John in Codex Sinaiticus, the oldest complete New Testament.

  1. 1
    c. 65 AD · Greek · Mark

    Mark 1:9-11: the baptism

    The Spirit descends into Jesus and the heavens are torn open.

  2. 2
    c. 80 AD · Greek · Matthew

    Matthew 5:48: "Be ye therefore perfect"

    The command to become teleios, like the Father.

  3. 3
    c. 90 AD · Greek · John

    John 1:1-3: "The Word was God"

    In the beginning was the Logos.

  4. 4
    c. 90 AD · Greek · John

    John 10:30-34: "I and my Father are one"; "Ye are gods"

    Accused of making himself God, Jesus answers with Psalm 82.

  5. 5
    c. 90 AD · Greek · John

    John 17:21: "That they all may be one"

    The prayer that extends divine unity to all believers.

  6. 6
    c. 90 AD · Greek · John

    John 1:14: "The Word was made flesh"

    Divinity enters humanity.
